# DREDWERKZ from its Steam Workshop item (2900215828), read as the file is (no converted copy). The map has no script
# for its lift or its pit: it uses teleporters. One takes a player to the low pad, a second one 1.6 m beside that spot
# takes him to the upper floor, a third takes who falls into the pit to a room under the map. AE's map volumes rule
# kills in a volume named as a kill zone ('Hell') on every map; its lifts do not act on a map read from MCC's files. The item is
# found in Steam's Workshop folder or in the game's own mcc_maps (STEAM ACCOUNT > WORKSHOP MAPS): the same name.
list dredwerkz-ws-11x
revision 1
title DREDWERKZ: THE WORKSHOP FILE

group workshop
map dredwerkz mcc debug flycam pm@ws2900215828
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0

test look
title DREDWERKZ from the Workshop: the map and its look
setup goto -21.2 1.0 -0.1
check The map is drawn: walls, lights and sky as they should be
check It plays as it does in MCC (note with F4 what differs)

test teleporters
title DREDWERKZ from the Workshop: its own teleporters
setup goto -15.0 9.3 2.2
check The first teleporter drops you on the low pad
check Step onto the pad to your right: it takes you upstairs
check You are not lifted by yourself while you stand still
expect 3 no log lifted from 'TPA'

test pit
title DREDWERKZ from the Workshop: the pit
setup goto -21.2 1.0 -0.1
check Walk off the ledge into the pit below
check You die in the pit (its 'Hell' volume kills)
expect 1 log fell into 'Hell'

# 1.0.0-rc2 CAMPAIGN: custom campaigns. A Halo 1 Workshop campaign installed from SETTINGS > SYSTEM > MCC > STEAM ACCOUNT
# > WORKSHOP MAPS (an MCC package with a CampaignInfo.json in mcc_maps) is listed in CAMPAIGN: its first row, CAMPAIGN,
# picks the stock campaign or a custom one by its title (A or left / right), and a custom one shows its MISSIONS in the
# manifest's order. A plays a mission at the difficulty on the right, alone or split screen. Each custom campaign keeps
# its own saved game, savegame-<package>.bin beside the stock savegame.bin in the profile's folder: its pause's SAVE AND
# QUIT writes it, its CONTINUE goes on from it, and the stock saved game stays as it was. Each test opens on its scene
# with DEV MODE's `menu campaign ... workshop [demo|fault|none|saved|damaged]`: workshop shows the first installed custom
# campaign (a made-up one, marked MADE-UP, when none is installed), fault a made-up fault line, none the page with no
# custom campaign, saved / damaged the made-up campaign with a made-up saved game (one to go on, one that cannot). The
# picker, play-quit, keep-stock and split tests need a Workshop campaign installed.
list campaign-workshop-rc2
revision 2
title 1.0.0-rc2 CAMPAIGN: CUSTOM CAMPAIGNS

test picker
title CAMPAIGN: the custom campaign and its missions
menus
setup menu campaign workshop
check CAMPAIGN row on top shows the Workshop campaign's title
check MISSIONS lists its missions; no HOST ONLINE
check Right side: mission, campaign, MISSION n OF n
expect 1 log ae campaign: staged:

test play-quit
title A custom mission: SAVE AND QUIT, then CONTINUE
menus
setup menu campaign workshop
check A on the mission; past a checkpoint, START, SAVE AND QUIT
check CAMPAIGN, the custom one: CONTINUE, MISSION 1 and difficulty
check A on CONTINUE: the mission goes on from that checkpoint
expect 1 log ae campaign: staged:

test keep-stock
title Each campaign's CONTINUE stays its own
menus
setup menu campaign
check CONTINUE (stock) is the stock level it was before
check A on it, START, SAVE AND QUIT: the menus come back
check The custom campaign's CONTINUE is as it was
expect 1 log ae campaign: open

test continue-look
title A custom campaign's CONTINUE row (made-up saved game)
menus
setup menu campaign workshop saved
check CONTINUE under CAMPAIGN: MISSION 2 and HEROIC
check Right side: SECOND MISSION, DIFFICULTY HEROIC
check Help line: Goes on from your saved game, at its difficulty.
expect 1 log ae campaign: staged:

test continue-fault
title A saved game that cannot go on says why
menus
setup menu campaign workshop damaged
check CONTINUE shows NOT AVAILABLE
check Its help: the saved mission's map is missing or damaged
check A on it does nothing; the MISSIONS still play
expect 1 log ae campaign: staged:

test split
title A custom mission in split screen
menus
setup menu campaign players 2 workshop

check A on the focused mission: two views; log: 2 players

test stock
title The stock campaign as before, under the picker
menus
setup menu campaign
check CAMPAIGN row shows HALO: COMBAT EVOLVED; CONTINUE, LEVELS
check Left / right on CAMPAIGN: the custom one, then back
check Left / right on a level still changes DIFFICULTY
expect 1 log ae campaign: open

test none

menus
setup menu campaign workshop none

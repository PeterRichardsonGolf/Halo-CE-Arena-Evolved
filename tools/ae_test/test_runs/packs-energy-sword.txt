# The energy sword pack (slot 14): given, kept through weapon switches.
# The switches are keys the run's input script presses (HALO_TEST_INPUT="menu: ... key:1 ...").
list packs-energy-sword
revision 1
title ENERGY SWORD PACK

group gulch
map bloodgulch
set debug.network_test_time_limit 10
setup goto start 0

test give-sword
title Energy sword in slot 14
setup give energy_sword
check The energy sword is in your hands
expect 1 weapon energy_sword

test keep-through-switch
title The sword stays through two switches
setup give energy_sword
check The energy sword is in your hands
check Press 1 twice: the sword is yours again
expect 1 weapon energy_sword
expect 2 weapon pistol

# The GRAVITY HAMMER pack, revision 3: on the back it lies along the spine (head low by the hip, pommel up past the
# shoulder) as another map's hammer does, a little out from the back, not through the body or the shoulder pads; on
# the ground it lies flat on its side, not standing in the floor. The hammer is the START weapon on every life
# (debug.network_test_cache_weapon 24) beside the gametype's BR; three dummies stand ahead (they stand still and never
# shoot), each holding its BR or the hammer, the other on its back; DEV MODE's give puts a pistol in your hands and
# drops the hammer you held, then you step back to look at it. The pack is held: this list runs only on a test build
# whose weapon cache holds it (without it the start weapon falls back to the assault rifle). F2 is DEV MODE's shoulder
# camera (your own back). Staged only: nothing here waits for something to happen.
list hammer-rc3
revision 2
title GRAVITY HAMMER: BACK AND GROUND

test back-behind
title Hammer on the back, from behind
setup goto 7.6 -3.2 0.6 facing 157
setup dummy 3 2.5 away
check A dummy with its BR in hand: the hammer lies along its back
check Head low by the hip; pommel clear of the shoulder pads
check Yours: Y to hold the BR, F2 to look: the same on you
map octagon_b
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 24

test back-side
title Hammer on the back, from the side
fresh
setup goto 7.6 -3.2 0.6 facing 157
setup dummy 3 2.5 away
setup goto 5.8 0.3 0.6 facing 247
check From the side the shaft lies flat on the back
check No part of the hammer goes through a chest or belly
map octagon_b
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 24

test drop-flat
title A dropped hammer on flat ground
fresh
setup goto 4.9 -2.2 0.6 facing 157
setup give pistol
setup goto 6.6 -2.9 0.6 facing 157
check The hammer lies flat on its side on the floor
check No part of it sinks into the floor or stands up
map octagon_b
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 24

test drop-slope
title A dropped hammer on a slope
setup goto 71.6 -170.0 1.8 facing -90
setup give pistol
setup goto 71.6 -167.5 2.0 facing -90
check On the hillside the hammer lies flat along the slope
check No part of it sinks into the ground or stands up
map bloodgulch
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 24

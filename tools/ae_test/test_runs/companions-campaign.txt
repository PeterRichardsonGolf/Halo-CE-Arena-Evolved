# CAMPAIGN AI SQUAD (DEV MODE `companion [<1-4>|off|reset|kill|revert|won]`, and the CAMPAIGN screen's AI SQUAD row): allied
# Spartans of a campaign level that the multiplayer bots' brain plays (game.companions_brain bot, the default; `actor` is the
# level's own AI on a leash, for a comparison; game.companions_skill is the bots' level, heroic by default). Each joins as a
# co-op player (a name, the team, the co-op spawn beside you) as the level starts, and takes its place beside you when co-op lets
# the players after the first spawn. Not in a level's intro: each appears behind you, out of view, on walkable ground, once you
# have control on foot (riding the intro's vehicle is kept for the levels whose intro is checked). The enemies are the
# level's AI: they aim by the bots' level, strafe, throw grenades, strike close up, stay 3.5-6 wu from you (a step further
# for each next one), come back to you when a fight takes them past 15 wu, come back after a death, ride in the free seats
# of a vehicle you sit in and get out when you do, and hold fire while you or another of them is in the line.
#
# The tests open at the level's start point: the level at first control after its intro, taken by the first load that plays
# the intro and read by the later ones (a few seconds, not the ride), with no squad in it: the squad joins in the level, as each
# test's `companion` line sends it. The b30-intro test starts the level from the CAMPAIGN screen and plays the real ride; a map
# test that wants the ride has the setup line `intro play`.
list companions-campaign
revision 8
title CAMPAIGN AI SQUAD

group b30
map b30
gametype coop

test b30-follow
title Silent Cartographer beach: they walk after you
setup companion 2
setup goto -62.4 20.7 1.6
check Spartans walk up the beach to you, no sliding
check They stop 3-6 steps from you, not on top of you
check Walk 10 steps away: they follow again
expect 1 log companion: 2 joined the game in progress

test b30-spread
title Three companions stand apart
setup companion 3
setup goto -62.4 20.7 1.6
check Three Spartans stand round you, not in one spot
check Turn and walk: they stay behind you, apart
expect 1 log companion: 3 joined the game in progress

test b30-fight
title Silent Cartographer beach: they fire at once
setup companion 2
setup goto -61.4 38.9 0.8
check Stay put: they fire the moment a Covenant is in sight
check They stay near you, not far up the beach
expect 1 log brain:

test b30-move
title They strafe and move in a fight
setup companion 2
setup goto -61.4 38.9 0.8
check In a fight they sidestep and shift, not rooted
check A fight draws them 15 steps off: they come back

test b30-grenade
title They throw grenades and strike close up
setup companion 2
setup goto -61.4 38.9 0.8
check A Spartan throws a grenade at a group or at cover
check A Covenant that reaches a Spartan is struck
expect 1 log blows

test b30-run
title They keep up when you run
setup companion 2
setup goto -62.4 20.7 1.6
check Run up the beach: both run after you, beside you
check Run back: they turn and follow at once

test b30-death
title A dead companion comes back
setup companion 2
setup companion kill
check Both Spartans fall, then stand beside a player again
check They follow and fight as before
expect 1 log companion: 2 joined the game in progress

test b30-warthog
title They ride in the Warthog and get out
set network.vehicle_riding true
setup goto -62.4 20.7 1.6
setup companion 2
setup spawn warthog here
setup seat driver
check You drive: a Spartan takes the gun, one the passenger seat
check Stop and get out: both get out and stand by you
expect 1 log companion: 1 takes seat

test b30-revert
title A checkpoint revert keeps them
setup companion 2
setup companion revert
check After the revert both Spartans are with you
check They follow and fight as before
expect 1 log companion: 2 joined the game in progress

group d20
map d20
gametype coop

test d20-spawn
title Keyes (no marines): companions still join
setup companion 2
check Two Spartans stand beside you
check Walk ahead: they follow you
expect 1 log companion: 2 joined the game in progress

group intro

test b30-intro
title Silent Cartographer: the squad enters after the intro
check No Spartan in the Pelican with you in the intro
check On the beach: they appear behind you, on the sand
check They are armed and follow; none stuck in the hull
expect 1 log companion: 1 enters behind the player
expect 1 no log spawned into seat

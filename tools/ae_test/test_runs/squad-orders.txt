# CAMPAIGN AI SQUAD, THE WHEEL'S ORDERS: in a campaign game the squad is off your leash under every order but the one
# that calls it back. MOVE, ATTACK, DEFEND and WATCH give each member a place at the order's point: it walks there and
# stays until your next order, however far you go; in a fight it fights about its place and walks back to it after.
# MOUNT sends the squad to a vehicle's seats (the gunner's first) and, with a member seated, gets it out. The squad's
# log says each member's place, who is in position, and every 5 seconds who is at their places and how far you are.
list squad-orders
revision 2
title CAMPAIGN AI SQUAD: THE WHEEL'S ORDERS

group b30
map b30
gametype coop
set debug.bot_events true

test move
retest 2
title MOVE: they walk to the spot and stay
fresh
setup companion 2
setup fireteam go ahead 12
check Both walk to the spot 12 steps ahead and stop there
check Walk far away from them: neither comes after you
check Call them back on the wheel: they come and follow
expect 1 log in position (2 of 2)
expect 2 no log let go of its going order
expect 2 no log the squad follows again

test attack
retest 2
title ATTACK: they push to the spot and stay
fresh
setup companion 2
setup fireteam attack ahead 15
check Both push to the spot 15 steps ahead
check They stay about it after, not back to you
expect 1 log squad order: attack: 
expect 2 log attack stands: 2 of 2

test defend
retest 2
title DEFEND: they hold the spot
fresh
setup companion 2
setup fireteam defend ahead 10
check Both go to the spot 10 steps ahead and hold it
check Out of a fight they stand still, apart
expect 1 log in position (2 of 2)
expect 2 log defend stands: 2 of 2
expect 1 log stands at its place

test watch
retest 2
title WATCH: they cover where you point, from where they are
fresh
setup companion 2
setup fireteam watch ahead 20
check Neither walks off: they turn to the place you gave
check Walk far away from them: they stay
expect 1 log squad order: watch: 
expect 2 log watch stands: 2 of 2

test mount
retest 2
title MOUNT: into the Warthog, and out again
fresh
setup companion 2
setup spawn warthog ahead
setup fireteam mount
check Both get into the Warthog, one on the gun
check MOUNT on the wheel again: both get out
expect 1 log is sent to the vehicle
expect 1 log mans seat

test flip
title MOUNT: a Warthog on its roof is turned over first
fresh
setup companion 2
setup spawn warthog flipped
setup fireteam mount
check A member walks to the upturned Warthog and turns it over
check Then both get in, one on the gun
expect 1 log flips the vehicle over
expect 2 log mans seat

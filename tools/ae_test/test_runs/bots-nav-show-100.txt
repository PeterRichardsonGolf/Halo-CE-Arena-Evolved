# THE BOTS' VEHICLE NAVIGATION DRAWN IN THE WORLD (DEV MODE): "nav show all" draws, over the ground near you, the routes the
# bots' drivers follow (ice-blue lines with squares at their points and an arrow mid-way along each leg), the rings round the
# rocks and trees their routes keep clear of, the ground closed to them, the places drivers crashed or wedged, and a line from
# each driving bot to the point it makes for. This list stands you at the west mouth of Danger Canyon looking down the
# lane, with the drawing on, so you can check the routes run clear of the rings and the drivers keep off the rocks and
# trees. Log at the start: "bot nav overlay: dangercanyon: N routes (N points), N obstacles, ...".

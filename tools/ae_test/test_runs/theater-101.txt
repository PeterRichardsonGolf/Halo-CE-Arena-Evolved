# The Theater's engine: a bot game recorded, then played back through a bot's eyes and a free camera, a seek and the
# end. The first group's game is the recording (network.record_replays on). The second group's map load (another
# map, no bots) ends it; each of its tests starts the newest recording again (replay play 1), seeks to 15 s, when
# the bots are out and fighting, and only then turns to its view (refused as "still seeking" till the seek is there).
# (Started from the menus, the menus stay drawn over the playback: the replay command does not close them.)
# The playback strip (a seek bar, pause and speed on screen) is not in this build: seek and views are DEV MODE's.
list theater-101
revision 1
title THEATER: RECORD A GAME, WATCH IT BACK

group record
map bloodgulch
set network.record_replays true

test record
title BLOOD GULCH: play a minute with the bots, then pass
setup bots 7 even normal
setup replay status
check Play about a minute: the game plays as usual
expect 1 cmd replay status ok

group watch
map chillout
set network.record_replays false
set bots.count 0

test eyes
title BLOOD GULCH replay at 15 s: a bot's eyes
setup replay play 1
setup replay seek 15
setup replay eyes 2
check Blood Gulch is drawn; you see as bot 2 sees
check The view glides as the world does, no stutter
check Bots move, shoot and die as they did
expect 1 log the postgame name its map, bloodgulch
expect 1 cmd replay eyes 2 ok
expect 1 no log replay: free camera: no player has a unit

test free
title BLOOD GULCH replay at 15 s: the free camera
setup replay play 1
setup replay seek 15
setup replay free
check The view is behind and above a player
check Blood Gulch is drawn around it, not a void
expect 1 log replay: free camera at
expect 1 no log no player has a unit
expect 2 log the postgame name its map, bloodgulch

test end
title BLOOD GULCH replay: seek to 50 s, then the end
setup replay play 1
setup replay seek 50
check It plays fast to 50 s (no seek bar in this build)
check It ends by itself; the menus are back, no error
expect 1 cmd replay seek 50 ok
expect 2 log ended at tick
expect 2 log replay: stopped

# rc2: the weapon spawners' HOLOGRAM on Sali Creek (h2d_salicreek, its layout applied). No rack and no plate: a returning
# weapon's outlined ghost lies where and how it will lie and fills as the timer runs; a power pad keeps its waypoint and
# time. Each test opens on its scene: the player stands by the spawner, DEV MODE's `spawner` stages the state and turns
# the player to look at the spot.
list spawners-rc2
revision 3
title Weapon spawners: the returning weapon's ghost

group comp
map h2d_salicreek
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set debug.weapon_set comp
set bots.count 0

test rack-filling
title A rack: its weapon's ghost fills as it returns
setup goto -6.7 -2.75 -1.35
setup spawner wait 40
check The weapon's outlined ghost lies on its spot, in view
check It fills stock to muzzle; at 0:00 the weapon lies in it

test pad-filling
title A power pad: the outline, the waypoint and its time
setup goto 1.2 6.4 -0.76
setup spawner wait 60
check Ghost fills; the waypoint has the name and time
check Walk 16+ units off: ghost gone, waypoint stays

test rack-ready
title A rack ready: no ghost
setup goto -6.7 -2.75 -1.35
setup spawner ready
check The weapon lies on its spot, no ghost

test rack-fresh-swap
title COMP: swap the same weapon for the rack's fresh one
setup goto -6.7 -2.75 -1.35
setup give battle_rifle
setup ammo 50
check The swap prompt shows with the rack's rifle (same weapon)
check Swap: the rifle in hand is full again
check The rack starts its timer: the ghost fills

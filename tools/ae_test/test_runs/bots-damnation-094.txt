# 0.9.4 DAMNATION (owner 11-15: "bots falling down big time", "spawn point pass ... where i am at"). The bots no longer
# make their own jumps across a gap with a deadly drop under it (Damnation's pit by the low walkway: 4 such jumps, 7
# bots dead in the pit in one game); a map that needs such a jump gets it as a hand `bot jump` line in its layout. The
# spawn flip (a team whose base is held spawns away from both flags) now needs 4 far points (Damnation has 2, side by
# side in the low corner, where both teams were sent into you), and a flipped spawn never ignores an enemy's sight.
# Revision 2 (after round 1, 11-15: 6 pit deaths left): a drop to a ledge is not taken when the ledge has a deadly drop
# ahead or all round (bots stepped off the upper level onto the hole's rim and ran on in); a bot at a jump's run-up no
# longer backs off a ledge; with fall damage on, a hurt bot treats a smaller drop as deadly (one died of an 8-unit drop).
# Revision 3 (after round 2, 11-16: 4 pit deaths, all one jump learnt from your own jump, upper level to the hole's rim): a
# learnt jump with a deadly drop under it is not used, and a jump a bot fell to its death from is off for that game.
# Revision 4 (11-16: "a group of blues just hanging out at bottom mid / rocket spawn"): the short jumps across bottom
# mid were taken for deadly drops (the probe went 6 units deep) and the bots there had no way out; the probe now goes
# as deep as the map's lethal drop, so only a gap a bot would die in is refused (the pit still is).
# Revision 5 (owner 11-16: "i don't think i have ever seen a bot successfully climb a ladder. I saw one start ... but was
# stuck at the bottom"): bots find the ladders from the level's climbable faces and climb them (face it, look up, hold
# forward); bottom mid's two ladders are ways out.
# Revision 6 (owner 11-16: a bot "left a perfectly good rocket launcher in the pit"; it went for the rockets from upstairs
# and gave them up at once as out of reach): bottom mid had no way in for the bots (only its ladders out), so they now drop
# into it off the bridge level's edge when they want something there.
# Revision 7 (0.9.4 freeze review): a jump a bot fell to its death off is rested a minute for every bot, and only a second
# such death takes it off for the game (one push or blast used to take it for good).
# Revision 8 (owner 11-16: "bots get the rockets but aren't using them now": 2 rockets fired in a game, both kills, none
# at targets 13 to 17 units off): a debug check. A bot with rockets that holds its fire now logs why (ev=hold_fire),
# so the next round shows what stops the far shots.

# 0.9.4 DAMNATION: bots and the pit. The bots do not make their own jumps across a gap with a deadly drop under it
# (Damnation's pit by the low walkway); a map that needs such a jump gets it as a hand `bot jump` line in its layout.
# The spawn flip (a team whose base is held spawns away from both flags) needs 4 far points (Damnation has 2, side by
# side in the low corner, where both teams were sent into you), and a flipped spawn never ignores an enemy's sight.
# A drop to a ledge is not taken when the ledge has a deadly drop ahead or all round; a bot at a jump's run-up does not
# back off a ledge; with fall damage on, a hurt bot treats a smaller drop as deadly.
# A learnt jump with a deadly drop under it is not used, and a jump a bot fell to its death from is off for that game.
# The short jumps across bottom mid are not taken for deadly drops: the probe goes as deep as the map's lethal drop, so
# only a gap a bot would die in is refused (the pit is).
# Bots find the ladders from the level's climbable faces and climb them (face it, look up, hold forward); bottom mid's
# two ladders are ways out.
# Bottom mid has a way in for the bots: they drop into it off the bridge level's edge when they want something there.
# A jump a bot fell to its death off is rested a minute for every bot, and only a second such death takes it off for the
# game.
# A bot with rockets that holds its fire logs why (ev=hold_fire).
# Bots pick up the rockets and fire them: the map's rockets lie on a scrap of floor the map's own path data joins to
# nothing, so the bots walk on to it from the floor beside it. NORMAL bots go for the rockets from 40 units and are
# there 3 s before they spawn; a power weapon lying within 10 units is taken in a fight too, a dropped one as well.
# Rockets fire while the aim is within the blast's reach of the feet, and a press that fired nothing is pressed again at
# once, not 2 s on. rocket-use hands an enemy bot the rockets (give rocket to bot); rocket-pickup watches the map's
# rockets at their first spawn (2:00).
# A bot going for an item takes the bots' links (ladders, drops, jumps): an item on another floor than the bot's takes
# the links' route there, teleporters aside; an entry left out of reach says why in its line, and a ladder taken or
# declined says so. rockets does not wait for a drop in (the pit has a way in off the small ladder's landing);
# small-ladder puts a bot at that ladder's foot.
list bots-damnation-094
revision 10
title DAMNATION: PIT JUMPS AND SPAWNS

group damnation
map damnation
gametype AE COMP TS
set bots.count 7
set bots.teams even
set bots.skill normal
set debug.bot_events true

test pit-falls
title DAMNATION: no bots into the pit
check Play 5 minutes near the low walkway by the pit
check No bot jumps or walks off into the pit there
expect 1 log bots: travel: jump not made

test bottom-mid
title DAMNATION: bots get out of bottom mid
check Watch bottom mid by the rockets and overshield
check Bots that go down there for them come back out
check No group stands there doing nothing

test ladders
title DAMNATION: bots climb the bottom-mid ladders
check Watch the two ladders at bottom mid
check Bots climb them, not stand at the bottom
expect 1 log climbed the ladder

test upper-rim
title DAMNATION: no drops from the upper level into the hole
check Play 5 minutes on the upper level above the floor hole
check No bot drops off it onto the rim and into the hole
check No bot backs off the ledge by the jump there

test pit-jump
title DAMNATION: no learnt jump over the hole
retest 7
check Fight bots on the upper level by the floor hole
check No bot jumps from there to the rim and falls in
expect 1 log has a deadly drop under it (not used)

test hurt-drops
title DAMNATION: hurt bots and big drops
check On the top level, shoot bots' shields down and chase
check Hurt bots run round, never die of a drop
check Bots with full health still drop down as before

test low-corner
title DAMNATION: the low corner's spawns
check Hold the low corner (where you sat 11-15) a while
check Bots stop spawning in front of you there
check Push into their base: still no spawns in your face

test rockets
retest 10
title DAMNATION: bots go down for the rockets
check Leave the rockets up at bottom mid; stay upstairs
check A bot goes down into bottom mid and takes them
expect 1 log goes for ROCKETS
expect 1 log item timers: 1 taken

test small-ladder
title DAMNATION: a bot climbs out by the small ladder
setup goto bot 2.8 5.9 0.1
check A bot is put at the small ladder's foot (bottom mid)
check It climbs out up a ladder within 20 s
check It does not stand at the foot or jump at it
expect 1 log climbed the ladder

test rocket-use
title DAMNATION: a bot fires the rockets it holds
retest 9
setup give rocket to bot
check The nearest enemy bot gets the rockets (a DEV line says so)
check Show yourself to it 10 or more units off, a few times
check It fires a rocket soon after it sees you, no holding
expect 1 log gave weapons\rocket launcher\rocket launcher to player
expect 1 log ev=shot target=0

test rocket-pickup
title DAMNATION: a bot goes for the map's rockets
check Play to 2:00 (the rockets' first spawn), near bottom mid
check A bot goes down for them and takes them within 20 s
check No bot stands under them or gives up at the edge
expect 1 log goes for ROCKETS
expect 1 log item timers: 1 taken

group damnation-dig
map damnation_dig
gametype AE COMP TS
set bots.count 7
set bots.teams even
set bots.skill normal
set debug.bot_events true

test dig-pit-falls
title DIGSITE DAMNATION: no bots into the pit
check Play 5 minutes near the pit
check No bot jumps or walks off into the pit

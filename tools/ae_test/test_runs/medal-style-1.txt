# MEDAL STYLE and the kill feed beside it, on testbox. UNIVERSAL is the default: a stack by your reticle, your points above it, a Killed
# row for each kill. MCC (Settings > HUD > MEDAL STYLE, each player's own, TEST RUN cannot set it) keeps the medals on the left and
# lifts the feed above them. HUD STYLE is each player's own too. The tests of one view, then of two, three and four views, share a map load each. The gaps under 8 px
# are what to note: nothing is hidden for them. Host and client, and an older build, are by hand: TEST RUN cannot join.

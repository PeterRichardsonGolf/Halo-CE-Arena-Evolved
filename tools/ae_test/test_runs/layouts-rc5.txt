# Weapon layouts with one weapon to an area (port/assets/ae/layouts.txt; no two weapons within 4 units): H2 FOUNDATION's
# middle pit now holds only the energy sword, and H2 ASCENSION (Steam Workshop item 3421353354, subscribe to it first) has
# a layout of its own: the pack's battle rifle on the map's two rifle spots and the bolt sniper on its two sniper spots,
# the needler, shotgun, plasma pistol and rockets as they were, its magnum, submachine gun and scarab gun out. Each test opens next to one spot in an AE game with
# AE PICKUPS on and no bots, so nothing takes a weapon before you see it. A spot that drops you into a hole or a wall: say so, the height was read from the map's
# own items, not from a floor you walked.
list layouts-rc5
revision 1
title WEAPON LAYOUTS: ONE WEAPON TO AN AREA

test foundation-pit
title H2 FOUNDATION: the middle pit
setup goto 10.9 -4.6 -1.43 facing -90
check The weapon in the middle of the pit is the ENERGY SWORD
check Nothing else lies in the pit: no shotgun, no pistol
check Walk over it: you take it
map h2d_foundation
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 1
set debug.network_test_pack_map_spawns true
expect 1 log the game's own is used

test ascension-rifle
title H2 ASCENSION: the rifle in the south
fresh
setup goto 3.0 -16.85 -0.05 facing 0
check The weapon ahead is the BATTLE RIFLE
check The plasma pistol further on is the map's own
check Walk over it: you take it
map h2-ascension@ws3421353354
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 1

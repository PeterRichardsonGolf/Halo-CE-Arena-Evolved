# 0.9.4 FRACTURED PEAK KING: bots take the hill. The top-middle hill's point sits over a gap in its floor: the goal is
# the floor beside the point at its height, up the ramp (not the floor 2.3 below it, which a bot walks under and never
# gets up from). The map's script kills anyone in a trigger volume under its cracks: two deaths in it (a second apart)
# mark it deadly, the bots keep out of it, the learnt file keeps it for the next games. In a hill game, your squad's
# bots on the hill are not walked back to you.
# A bot holding the hill that fights keeps its moves inside (along the edge, or back to the middle), and its hold lasts
# a moment past a step out.
# The squads sent to the hill take the hill's own level (beside its point, else its markers), not the floor under the
# top-middle hill's centre.
# A trigger volume is learnt deadly after 3 map kills of different players, none while a round's countdown runs, so a
# reset or a spawn's telefrag does not mark it; the cracks are learnt a little later.

# TEST ROUND 11-11 (AE Latest's combined build: the 0.8.1 line, the MODERN ragdoll pushes, the 0.9.0 bots and the death
# and follow cameras). Tests with the same map follow each other and share one load.
# TEMPLE 2v2 (you and one bot against two):
# - BOT LEVELS: every level from EASY to DEMON evenly spaced; HEROIC easier than 0.8.1, ELITE about the old HEROIC,
#   LEGENDARY a little harder. Play each a couple of minutes; the steps should feel about equal.
# - GRENADES: bots now get out of the way of your grenades too (not only their own); better levels more often.
# - STANDING STILL FIXED: a bot hunting the player who keeps killing it no longer stands under them a floor below;
#   camo and overshield first appear 2 minutes in.
# - CAMERAS: dying, the camera eases out from your eyes to your body, your killer in view; the right stick orbits it; it
#   stays until respawn or A, LB or RB (from 3 s in). The follow camera sits behind the player and turns with their aim;
#   a change of player cuts with a quick fade in. (display.follow_camera "always" here.)
# PRISONER and CHILL OUT: the 0.9.0 bot gaps (no spawn grace on HEROIC, high ground, no short cuts mid-fight, weapon by
# range). OCTAGON: the four ragdoll styles on the same BR kill (the AI row NONE, so a wrong pick shows stock).
list round-1111
revision 4
title TEST ROUND 11-11: BOTS, CAMERAS, RAGDOLLS

group temple
map temple
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set bots.count 3
set bots.teams even
set bots.skill heroic
set display.follow_camera always
set debug.bot_events true

test temple-easy
title TEMPLE 2V2: EASY bots
setup bots 3 even easy
setup goto start 0
check EASY: beatable, but it fights back
expect 1 log skill=easy
check How soon a bot hits you; do you win 1v1s
check Bots move and dodge like players, not robots

test temple-heroic
title TEMPLE 2V2: HEROIC bots

check Step from EASY: about the same as the others

test temple-still
title TEMPLE: no bot stands still for long
setup bots 3 even heroic
setup goto start 0
check Kill one bot a few times, stand on the platform
check It doesn't stand under you; it moves on
check Camo and overshield (2 min in): bots go for them

test camera-death-hold
title CAMERA: dying, eyes out to your body, killer in view
setup bots 3 even normal
setup goto start 0
setup kill me
check Eases out from your eyes; looks at your killer
check Right stick orbits your body; it stays till respawn
check Press A after 3 s: a quick fade in on a teammate

test camera-follow-switch
title CAMERA: LB and RB switch with a quick fade
setup kill me
check LB or RB: a quick fade in on the next one
check It turns with their aim; jumps and crouches don't bob
check On respawn you are back in your own view

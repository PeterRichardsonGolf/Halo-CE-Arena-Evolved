# 0.9.3 BOTS: the can't-reach report (dummy reach). Four walking dummies on your team walk from every spawn of the map
# to every flag stand, ball spawn, hill, race checkpoint and power item, by the bots' own routes, never shooting. Each
# walk's line is in the log ("dev reach: ... reached in 23 s", or stuck, lost, slow, died and where). At the end the
# report is written to the config folder's reach/<map>.txt. A full run takes about 10-20 minutes: you can watch, fly or
# leave it. The walks that fail are the map spots to fix.

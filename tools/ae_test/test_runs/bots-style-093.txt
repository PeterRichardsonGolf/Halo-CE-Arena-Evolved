# 0.9.3 BOTS: play styles (DEV botstyle; the offline social career gives each bot name its own). A style bends what a bot
# chooses in a fight, mildly: PUSHER is bolder (retreats later, charges more), jumps a little more and halts less;
# ANCHOR is the other way; HOPPER jumps the most. Its aim and reaction stay its skill's. Two HEROIC enemy bots, you
# cannot die (god). The log names each bot's style once a game: "bots: style: bot 1 PUSHER (aggression +2 ...)".
list bots-style-093

title BOTS: PLAY STYLES

group temple
map temple
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0

test pusher-anchor
title TEMPLE: a PUSHER and an ANCHOR at once
setup botstyle clear
setup botskill clear
setup bots 2 against heroic
setup botstyle 1 pusher
setup botstyle 2 anchor
setup god me
check Bot 1 presses you and backs off late
check Bot 2 holds back, pauses more, retreats early
expect 1 log bots: style: bot 1 PUSHER

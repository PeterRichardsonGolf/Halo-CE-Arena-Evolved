# FORGE, step 5 (AI): an AI spawn is a marker the level turns into a squad when the game starts. `forge ai <actor> <team> [goal [seconds]]` places one 5
# world units ahead of the Monitor (the actor is a part of its variant's name, the team covenant for an enemy and players for ALL PLAYERS, a friend of
# every player; the goal none, patrol, route or hunt, the seconds the delay of its RESPAWN), `forge route <points>` an AI route, `forge volume patrol`
# an AI PATROL volume; `forge mode play` after them waits for them and plays the AI from its markers.

revision 4
title FORGE: AI

# 0.9.0: bots shoot down hung items (powerups, a better weapon, a health pack while hurt). Digsite's workshop maps (Steam item 3477809506) hang some powerups in the air;
# one bullet brings one down. A bot that sees no enemy and took no hit for 2 s shoots at a hung powerup in its reach
# and sight: it stands, aims, taps once, waits half a second; after 4 s at one item it leaves it 20 s. Then the bots
# go for the fallen item. Revision 2 (owner 11-09): any wanted item, not only the power items: Chill Out's
# hung sword (chillout_dig) is the weapon test. With debug.bot_events the log has "ev=hung_shoot" and "ev=hung_left" lines.
# Before the round: the Digsite Multiplayer pack subscribed in Steam (MCC), so its maps are in AE's map list.
list bots-powerup-090
revision 2
title BOTS: SHOOTING DOWN HUNG ITEMS

group hotbox
map hotbox_dig@ws3477809506
gametype AE FFA SLAY
set bots.count 3

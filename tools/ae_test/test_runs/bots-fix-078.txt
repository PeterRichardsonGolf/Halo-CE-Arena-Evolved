# The bot fix round of 0.7.8 (owner run 2 of bots-vantage-078): NEED A RIDE from a damaged vehicle's seat, fireteams split by
# scale and free with no order, tanks in Slayer, vantage holds for any squad member, and DEV MODE's "why" lines (a bot that
# drops or refuses what it was asked says why on the HUD; the log has "bots: why:"). `call ride` is the wheel's NEED A RIDE.
# Headless: run_list.py with --after 90; the bot scorecard (tools/ae_test/bot_scorecard.py) judges the counts.
# Revision 2 (owner run 3: regroup and ride-damaged FAIL): REGROUP is held in a big team game until the next order, the
# leader's death or REGROUP again once formed; the reset before each test ends the squad's orders; its setup lines now run
# once the bots the reset put back are up (`call ride` had found none).
list bots-fix-078
revision 2
title BOTS: THE FIX ROUND

group gulch
map bloodgulch
gametype AE BTB SLY
set bots.count 15
set bots.skill heroic
set debug.bot_events true

test free-team
title With no order your squad plays on its own
check Your squad has 3 or 4 bots, the rest form squads
check With no order they play on their own, not trailing you
check The status line names your squad's size
setup fireteam status

test regroup
title REGROUP brings them and they keep firing
setup fireteam regroup
check The dev command is taken
expect 1 cmd fireteam regroup ok
check Your squad comes to you, shooting as they come
check They stay close by you until your next order

test regroup-again
title REGROUP again lets them play free
setup fireteam regroup
check Once they came the HUD says: with you until next order
check REGROUP on the wheel with them by you: they play free
check The HUD says: squad: playing free

test ride-damaged
title NEED A RIDE from a damaged warthog
setup goto start 0
setup spawn warthog ahead
setup seat gunner
setup call ride
check The call is taken
expect 1 cmd call ride ok
check A bot comes to drive, even with the hull damaged
check If no one can come, the HUD says why

test tanks-holds
title Tanks and high ground in Slayer
setup goto start 0
check A bot takes a tank on its own and drives it
check A bot with a sniper or rockets holds a high spot
check A bot that drops a ping says why on the HUD

group temple
map temple
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set bots.count 7
set bots.teams even
set bots.skill heroic
set debug.bot_events true

test arena-near
title Arena: your squad stays loosely near
check With no order your squad stays within about 25 units
check They do not trail you step by step

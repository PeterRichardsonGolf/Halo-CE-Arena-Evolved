# COMBAT FEEDBACK's early shield pop shows when one more headshot of the weapon in use finishes: the least of 25 points
# and one of its hits against the shield. On Octagon 2 with a dummy that stands still (75 shield, 75 health): the
# magnum pops after 2 hits (25 left), the sidekick after 5 (4.8 left, not after 4), the DMR after 3 rounds (18.2 left),
# the EVO RIFLE (nothing past a breaking shield) only as its 4th round breaks it. Shoot the head each time. A dummy
# heals to full 3 s after it was last hurt, so wait that long between tries. A give puts the weapon in hand.
list pop-weapon-080
revision 1
title SHIELD POP BY WEAPON

group octagon-cache
map octagon_b
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 1
set debug.objective_log full

test pop-magnum
title Magnum: the pop after the 2nd hit, the 3rd kills
setup give weapons\pistol\pistol
setup dummy 1 10
setup ttk on
check 1st head hit: no pop
check 2nd head hit: the pop shows, it lives
check 3rd head hit: it dies
expect 1 cmd dummy ok

test pop-sidekick
title Sidekick: the pop after the 5th hit, not the 4th
setup give sidearm
setup dummy 1 10
setup ttk on
check 4 head hits: no pop yet, the dummy lives
check 5th head hit: the pop shows, it lives
check 6th head hit: it dies
expect 1 cmd dummy ok

test pop-dmr
title DMR: the pop after the 3rd round, the 4th kills
setup give dmr
setup dummy 1 10
setup ttk on
check 2 head rounds: no pop yet
check 3rd head round: the pop shows, it lives
check 4th head round: it dies
expect 1 cmd dummy ok

test pop-evo
title EVO RIFLE: the pop as the 4th round breaks it
setup give evo
setup dummy 1 10
setup ttk on
check 3 head rounds: no pop, the dummy lives
check 4th head round: the pop shows, it lives
check 5th head round: it dies
expect 1 cmd dummy ok

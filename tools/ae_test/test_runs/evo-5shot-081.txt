# The EVO RIFLE kills with five rounds to the head (the fifth); the DMR stays at four (the fourth). On Octagon 2 with a
# dummy that stands still: the EVO's 4th round breaks the shield (the pop shows) and carries nothing past it, the 5th
# to the head kills.
# The DMR's 4th round breaks the shield and a head hit kills. A dummy heals to full 3 s after it was last hurt, so wait
# that long between tries. The rifles are the weapon cache's weapons (7 the EVO RIFLE, 6 the DMR); a give puts the weapon
# in hand.
list evo-5shot-081
revision 1
title EVO RIFLE 5 SHOT / DMR 4 SHOT

group octagon-cache
map octagon_b
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0
set debug.network_test_cache_weapon 7
set debug.objective_log full

test evo-5shot
title EVO RIFLE: the fifth round to the head kills
setup give evo
setup dummy 1 10
setup ttk on
check 3 head rounds: no pop, the dummy lives
check 4th head round: the pop shows, it lives
check 5th head round: it dies
expect 1 cmd dummy ok

test dmr-4shot
title DMR: the fourth round to the head kills
setup give dmr
setup dummy 1 10
setup ttk on
check 3 head rounds: the dummy lives, no pop yet
check The 4th head round: the pop shows and it dies
expect 1 cmd dummy ok

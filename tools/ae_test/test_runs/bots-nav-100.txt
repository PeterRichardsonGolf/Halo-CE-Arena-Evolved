# 1.0 rc2 BOT NAV: a map may carry a hand-placed nav file of vehicle routes (lanes base to base, base exits, crossings;
# scorpion lines of their own) and keepouts (rocks, trees, steep ledges every driver's route goes round), placed with
# the nav tools. A driver with a far goal follows the routes, the usual search after their end. Blood Gulch is the first
# map with one; a map without a file plays as before (setting bots.nav, on). Log: "bot nav: bloodgulch (built in): 14
# routes (74 points), 18 keepouts" at the load, and with bot events "bot nav: bot N on route <name>".
# A bot that sits at a Warthog's wheel with the gun empty honks (GET IN), a teammate bot near by goes for the gun and
# the driver waits up to 10 s for it; a human teammate heading at the vehicle counts from 20 units and is waited for up
# to 10 s.
list bots-nav-100
revision 2
title BOT NAV: BLOOD GULCH ROUTES

group bloodgulch
map bloodgulch@mcc
gametype AE BTB CTF
set bots.count 12
set debug.bot_events true

test lanes
title Blood Gulch: drivers keep to the lanes
check Ride with or watch the warthogs and ghosts a while
check They drive the valley lanes, not into rocks or walls
check None sits stuck against a rock or ledge for long
expect 1 log bot nav: bloodgulch (built in)
expect 1 log on route

test exits
title Blood Gulch: drivers get out of the bases
check Watch both bases' vehicles as bots take them
check They drive out round the base walls, no wedging
check The scorpions get out too, slower lines

test scorpion
title Blood Gulch: a tank crosses the map
check Watch a bot drive a scorpion toward the other base
check It keeps off the west wall's steep ledge
check It reaches the far side without backing in circles

test gunner
title Blood Gulch: a bot driver waits for its gunner
check Walk to a Warthog a bot has just got in (20 u)
check It honks and waits; you get in the gun seat
check Its gun is manned more often than it drives alone

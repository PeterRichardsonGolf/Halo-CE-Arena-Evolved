# FORGE, step 4 (volumes and map settings): a volume is a box the world's physics and damage ask about: a GRAVITY volume pulls what is in it
# (players, vehicles, items, grenades, ragdolls) at its STRENGTH times the world's gravity, a KILL volume kills what is in it, a DAMAGE one hurts it,
# a ONE WAY slab is a floor from above. MAP SETTINGS sets what the map's players are (a team's biped, its atmosphere). `forge volume <kind> [value]`
# makes a volume where the Monitor's feet would stand (gravity: a 12 x 12 x 40 column from the ground up, value the strength in hundredths, 25 =
# a quarter); the volume and the settings are made once the Monitor flies, and `forge mode play` after them waits for them. `forge settings biped red
# (the Spartan spawns at one of the map's spawns, so each volume test then puts it where the volume is: `goto`).
# other` sets RED's biped to the level's other biped; `forge testspawn red` is MAP SETTINGS' TEST SPAWN, begun when the Monitor is left.
list forge-s4
revision 6
title FORGE: VOLUMES AND MAP SETTINGS

test gravity
retest 6
title GRAVITY volume (25): a jump goes high and comes down slowly
check Jump in the column: you rise far above a normal jump
check You come down slowly
check Walk out of the column: gravity is the world's again
expect 1 log forge dev: volume gravity as

expect 1 no log cannot be read

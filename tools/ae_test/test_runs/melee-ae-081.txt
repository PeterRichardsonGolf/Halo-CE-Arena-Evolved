# The MELEE rule (GAME OPTIONS, SPARTAN: MELEE, CLASSIC / MODERN) on Octagon 2 with a dummy that stands still: MODERN's
# melee is a finisher. A melee blow is worth at least a third more than a player's full health (100 at the stock 75, and
# it follows the gametype's health and resistance); it kills a player whose shield is down or nearly down outright (a
# leftover goes on to health), always from behind, and COMBAT FEEDBACK's shield pop shows when a melee can finish. Reach
# is the same for every weapon. CLASSIC is Halo CE's melee as before.
# Two groups share one map load each (the stock weapons; the weapon cache's, for the battle rifle). Every test sets the
# rule itself (option melee modern|classic) and puts the weapon its checks name in hand (a give puts the new weapon in
# hand and, with two held, drops the one that was in hand, so a test gives one weapon, its last); no menu work. The
# record of each hit is the ev=ps hp and shield lines (set debug.objective_log full); a dummy heals to full 3 s after it
# was last hurt, so wait that long between tries. The magnum is the stock pistol; the battle rifle is the weapon cache's
# weapon 1.
# Revision 3 (0.9.4, 11-16: MELEE ships in 0.9.4; the same tests on the 0.9.4 build).
# Revision 4 (owner 11-16: melee-mate SKIP, "team mate did not spawn could not test"): the mate dummy joined the other team
# (bots.teams even puts a lone bot opposite you); "dummy ... mate" now puts it on your team.
list melee-ae-081
revision 4
title MELEE RULE: CLASSIC / MODERN

group octagon-stock
map octagon_b
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 0
set debug.objective_log full

test melee-pistol
title MELEE MODERN with the magnum: shots, then the blow
setup option melee modern
setup give weapons\pistol\pistol
setup dummy 1 1.0
check 2 magnum shots, then melee: the dummy dies
expect 1 cmd option melee modern ok
check Melee at full shield: it does not kill
check Melee again, shield now down: the second blow kills

title MELEE MODERN: the shield pop with the magnum
setup option melee modern

# BOTS: an arcing projectile and a jumping target. A bot aims above the target by its projectile's fall over the
# flight, from the projectile's own speeds, slowing range and air gravity (the fuel rod gun: about 0.7 units higher at
# 10 units, 4.8 at 25, 13.9 at 40), at every level; its aim error stays its level's. While the target is in the air a
# bot keeps a share of its lead by level: RECRUIT and EASY none, NORMAL half along the ground and none up, HEROIC three
# quarters, ELITE and up all. HEROIC and up lead a moving target by the shot's whole flight; the lower levels
# by at most 1 s of it. One enemy bot holds the fuel rod gun (give plasma_cannon to bot); you cannot die (god).
# The bots' shots log with their distance (debug.bot_events: "ev=shot ... dist="). testbox (a map with the fuel rod gun),
# so that each test opens on its distance: the bot in one corner, you in the far one 21 units off or 9 units off, facing
# it. The bot keeps the
# fuel rod gun (an energy weapon: no rounds to run out of), and switches weapons 3 s apart at most, each switch logged
# with its reason ("ev=weapon_switch why=").
list bots-rc6
revision 4
title BOTS: FUEL ROD ARC, JUMPING TARGETS

group testbox
map testbox@ce
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set bots.count 0
set debug.bot_events true

test rod-heroic
title HEROIC: the fuel rod reaches you at range
setup bots 1 against heroic
setup god me
setup give plasma_cannon to bot
setup goto bot -7.5 -6.8 0.5
setup goto 6.0 9.3 0.5 facing 230
check At 21 units, stand still: its rods land on you
check Strafe along the wall: the rods still reach you
check It keeps the fuel rod, no flicking to another
expect 1 log gave weapons\plasma_cannon\plasma_cannon to player
expect 3 no log why=empty

test rod-close
title HEROIC: the fuel rod at 10 units
fresh
setup bots 1 against heroic
setup god me
setup give plasma_cannon to bot
setup goto bot -7.5 -6.8 0.5
setup goto -1.5 -0.5 0.5 facing 227
check At 9-10 units: it still hits, none fly high
check It keeps the fuel rod, no flicking to another
expect 1 log gave weapons\plasma_cannon\plasma_cannon to player
expect 2 no log why=empty

test jump-normal
title NORMAL: a side jump dodges the rod
fresh
setup bots 1 against normal
setup god me
setup give plasma_cannon to bot
setup goto bot -7.5 -6.8 0.5
setup goto 6.0 9.3 0.5 facing 230
check Side-jump as it fires: rods miss behind
check Stand still: its rods still land on you
expect 1 log gave weapons\plasma_cannon\plasma_cannon to player

test jump-elite
title ELITE: it catches you mid-jump
fresh
setup bots 1 against elite
setup god me
setup give plasma_cannon to bot
setup goto bot -7.5 -6.8 0.5
setup goto 6.0 9.3 0.5 facing 230
check Side-jump as it fires: it catches you
check Jump in place: it still hits you
check Run across its line: its rods meet you
expect 1 log gave weapons\plasma_cannon\plasma_cannon to player

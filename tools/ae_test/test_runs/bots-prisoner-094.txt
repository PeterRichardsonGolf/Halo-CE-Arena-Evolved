# 0.9.4 PRISONER: bots and drops. A bot in a fight with its shields under three quarters takes no drop of a floor's
# height (a ramp still walks), and a retreat backs off to a place on its own level when there is one, so a shot bot on
# the top floor falls back into its rooms.
# A hurt bot (NORMAL and up) retreats to a covered place you cannot see and holds there until its shields are back;
# HEROIC and up keep that place in mind as they fight and back off towards it. A bot whose magazine runs dry in a fight
# takes its other weapon when you are near, else reloads.
# A hurt bot falls back at a low confidence (NORMAL under 0.2, HEROIC 0.3, ELITE and up 0.35; HEROIC and up as its
# shields pop while losing), and a bot in a fight hops up after you onto a crate or step a jump high.
# No bot takes a drop of a floor's height or more (top floor to the ground, 3.6), fighting or not, whatever its shields;
# a chase hop crouch-jumps, so it reaches a crate's top.
list bots-prisoner-094
revision 4
title PRISONER: SHOT BOTS KEEP THE HIGH GROUND

group prisoner
map prisoner
gametype AE COMP TS
set bots.count 7
set bots.teams even
set bots.skill heroic
set debug.bot_events true

test top-floor
title PRISONER: no bots jump off the top floor
retest 4
check From the ground floor, shoot bots on the top floor
check A shot bot backs into a top room, not down to you
check Full shields or not, none jump down to the ground floor
expect 1 log kept=level

test cover-retreat
title PRISONER: hurt bots fall back to cover
retest 4
check Pop a bot's shields: it backs into a doorway or room
check It waits there out of your sight, then comes back
check HEROIC bots back off towards it as they fight
expect 1 log cover=1

test empty-magazine
title PRISONER: no fighting with an empty gun
check Make a bot empty its magazine at you close up
check It takes its other weapon at once (or reloads far off)
check Never stands at you with an empty gun
expect 1 log bots: ev=empty_

test chase-hop
title PRISONER: bots hop up after you
retest 4
check Fight a bot, then jump up onto a crate near it
check It jumps up after you, not down off the level
expect 1 log kind=chase

# DEV MODE unseen: "unseen [me|<player>|off]" hides a player from the bots. They do not see, target or hunt that player,
# and do not count them as an enemy near; a bot they shoot reacts to the hurt (cover, unscoping) but never turns on them.
# A new game, a reset or "unseen off" ends it. Log: "dev: unseen on for <name>" and "dev: unseen off".
list dev-unseen-100
revision 2
title DEV MODE: UNSEEN BY THE BOTS

group unseen
map bloodgulch
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set bots.count 6
set bots.skill heroic

test unseen-walk
title Walk up to the bots unseen
setup unseen me
check Walk among the enemy bots: none shoots or turns to you
check Bots on your team still play as before
expect 1 cmd unseen me ok

test unseen-off
title Unseen off: the bots see you again
setup unseen off
check Stand in view of an enemy bot: it fights you again
expect 1 cmd unseen off ok

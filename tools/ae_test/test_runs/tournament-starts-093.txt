# 0.9.3 AE TOURNAMENT: mixed starts and AE's weapons. A game of a casual playlist starts with the battle rifle or the evo rifle
# (picked by weight from the game's seed), a COMP playlist with the evo rifle or the CE pistol, and the tournament plays AE's
# weapons whatever WEAPON PACKS says (put back after the game). CLASSIC COMP plays the maps' own weapons. The DEV word `variant
# br|evo|magnum|none` after `social start` names the start so each check opens on it. Nothing waits for a game to happen.
list tournament-starts-093
revision 2
title TOURNAMENT STARTS

test variant-lobby
title The lobby names the start
check Under the map: 4 V 4, BATTLE RIFLE, AE WEAPONS
check Press A: the game loads with the battle rifle in hand
menus
setup social maps hide smp
setup social start team-slayer map prisoner variant br
expect 1 log ae social lobby: open team-slayer

title Evo start, AE weapons

check The post-game line ends with EVO RIFLE

expect 1 weapon evo

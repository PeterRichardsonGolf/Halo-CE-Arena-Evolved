# CAMPAIGN AI SQUAD, PLAYED: the squad as it walks with you. Each member walks to a place behind you picked on the ground
# about you (out of doors' way), or on your own track indoors, over your jumps one at a time and down your drops; it steps
# aside or walks on ahead when you come its way, lines up beside a closed door you wait at (every door here) and goes in
# when you go through, and comes back to you only when it is stuck out of sight. Each test starts you some way before the
# part it checks: walk in as you would play. The a10 start plays the level's intro, so the squad is with you from the start
# as in a game; the doors test starts from the saved point after Keyes (core/point-keyes.bin): walk out of the bridge and
# through the first door that opens for you. Bot events are on: the squad logs where you and each member walk every half
# second, so a later test can walk your own way.
list squad-port-play
revision 5
title CAMPAIGN AI SQUAD: PLAYED

group a10
map a10
gametype coop
set game.campaign_skip_training true
set game.companions_door_stack every
set debug.bot_events true

test a10-start
title Pillar of Autumn: out of cryo and over the pipes
fresh
setup intro play
setup companion 3
check They follow you out, apart, not on top of you
check Over the pipes one at a time, none warps
check Turn back past them: they let you by

test a10-doors
title Pillar of Autumn: the first door after Keyes
fresh
setup point load keyes
setup companion 2
check Wait at the first shut door: they line up by it
check Walk through it: they follow, none left behind
check Neither stands in a doorway or in your way

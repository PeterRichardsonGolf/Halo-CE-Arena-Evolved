# The bots' wheel calls (0.9.1): bot teammates mark what they already act on, as a human teammate's call does (ENEMY on
# a shared sighting, SMART on a power item, GOING on a flank, DEFEND on a flag stand or hill, RIDE from a driver waiting
# for riders, REGROUP while a crew walks its route spread apart). Only teams with a person get calls; bots.calls (on)
# turns them off. Every test sets itself up. Headless: run_list.py --after 90 --link packs=<the data root's packs> (an
# AE gametype after the first map needs the weapon packs).
list bots-calls-091

title BOTS: WHEEL CALLS

group ctf

gametype AE BTB CTF

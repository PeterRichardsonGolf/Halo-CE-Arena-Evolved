# The own flag's waypoint WHEN SPOTTED (the AE CTF seeds): away from home it shows; carried by an enemy it shows to the defenders
# only while one of them has a line of sight to the carrier, and 1.5 s after. Each test opens on its scene from its setup lines:
# you stand in the open in front of your base, looking at its door (a player start's point), and the carrier is a runner
# (dummy run) that already runs your flag on a fixed path, left of the card. The base is the host's own in a local game. debug.waypoint_log says each change
# in the log ("flag spotted: ...", "own flag waypoint: ...").
# The third test keeps a runner in the other base, out of the way: a team left empty ends the game in 60 s.
list flag-spotted
revision 3
title FLAG WHEN SPOTTED

group ctf
map beavercreek
gametype AE CTF
set bots.count 0
set debug.waypoint_log true

test carrier-seen
title Enemy runs your flag out of your base and back in
setup goto 11.38 18.55 -0.22 facing -40
setup dummy run 27.8 13.5 -0.2 to 15.9 16.3 -0.3 loop
setup flag ours carry
check The carrier runs your flag out of your base, and back
check In view: a waypoint over him
check Behind the walls: the waypoint goes 1.5 s later
expect 1 cmd flag ours carry ok
expect 2 log flag carrier seen

test carrier-hidden
title Enemy runs your flag behind your base's walls
setup goto start 4
setup dummy run 27.9 17.0 -0.2 to 27.8 10.6 -0.2 loop
setup flag ours carry
check He runs your flag inside your base, out of sight
check No waypoint for your flag: nobody sees its carrier
expect 1 cmd flag ours carry ok
expect 2 no log flag carrier seen

test flag-dropped
title Your flag dropped: its waypoint shows without sight
setup goto start 4
setup dummy run 0.3 10.5 -0.2 to 0.4 16.9 -0.2 loop
setup flag ours stand
check Your flag lies loose in your base, walls between
check Its waypoint shows from here: a loose flag needs no sight
expect 1 cmd flag ours stand ok

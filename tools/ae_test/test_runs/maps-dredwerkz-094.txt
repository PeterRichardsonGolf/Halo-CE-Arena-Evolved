# 0.9.4 DREDWERKZ: the pit and the lifts. The map names its pit's floor "Hell" and a lift "TPA" to "TPB" but its own
# script uses neither. AE kills anyone who falls into a volume named as a kill zone (the host's), puts a player who
# steps into a lift volume TP<letter> at the next letter's (TPA to TPB), and the bots take the kill zone for deadly from
# the map's load, so they never walk into the pit. Test launches leave the playlist alone, so the test plays Dredwerkz,
# not a playlist's UP NEXT.
list maps-dredwerkz-094
revision 2
title DREDWERKZ: THE PIT KILLS, THE LIFT LIFTS

group dredwerkz
map dredwerkz@smp
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 7
set bots.teams even
set bots.skill heroic
set debug.bot_events true

test pit
title DREDWERKZ: the pit kills
retest 2
setup goto -21.2 1.0 -0.1
check Walk off the ledge into the pit below
check You die as you land in it
expect 1 log fell into 'Hell'

test lift
title DREDWERKZ: the lift takes you up
retest 2
setup goto -15.0 9.3 2.2
check Take the teleporter you stand on
check You come out on the upper floor, not the low pad
expect 1 log lifted from 'TPA'

test bots-pit
title DREDWERKZ: no bots in the pit
retest 2
check Play a few minutes
check No bot goes down into the pit
expect 1 log is the map's kill zone

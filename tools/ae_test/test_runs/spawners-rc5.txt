# 1.0 rc5 WEAPON SPAWNERS: GAME OPTIONS > WEAPONS > WEAPON SPAWNS (shown while AE WEAPONS is on) picks RACKS, the
# spawners' holograms with each spot on its set's logic; CLASSIC + HOLOGRAMS, plain weapons on fixed timers with the
# ghost of a spot's weapon while it is away; or CLASSIC, the same with no holograms. RANDOMIZE (RACKS only) draws a
# rack's weapon every game, every round or every spawn. Two weapons the map lays together are one spot (Wizard's two
# pistols). On RACKS a rack's fresh weapon gives no walk-over ammo to a player holding the same one. Logs: a hologram's
# "drawn (ghost at", "look classic)", "look classic + holograms)", "(RANDOMIZE every game".
list spawners-rc5
revision 4
title WEAPON SPAWNERS: RACKS, CLASSIC, RANDOMIZE

group wizard
map h1pb_wizard
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set debug.weapon_set casual
set bots.count 0

test merged-spot
title Wizard: the two pistols lie as one spot
setup goto -7.6 7.6 -2.75 facing 315
check One weapon lies where the map put two pistols
check Take it: one ghost fills on that spot
expect 1 log merges item 5

test racks
title RACKS: a taken weapon's ghost fills as it returns
setup goto -3.35 3.07 -2.75 facing 120
setup spawner wait 40
check The ghost lies on the floor where the weapon lay
check Walk round it: it stays, never in the air
check It fills; at 0:00 the weapon lies in it
expect 1 log : drawn (ghost at

test dropped
title RACKS: the ghost shows over a dropped weapon
setup goto -3.35 3.07 -2.75 facing 120
setup spawner wait 40
check Drop your weapon on the empty spot
check The ghost still shows over what you dropped
check It fills; at 0:00 the rack's weapon lands
expect 1 log : drawn (ghost at

test walkover
title RACKS: a rack's fresh weapon gives no walk-over ammo
setup goto -3.35 3.07 -2.75 facing 120
check Take the rack's weapon; wait for the next
check Walk over the new one: no ammo, it stays
check Hold action on it: you swap for the fresh one

test every-game
title RANDOMIZE EVERY GAME: a rack keeps its weapon
fresh
setup option randomize every_game
setup goto -3.35 3.07 -2.75 facing 120
check Take the weapon on this rack
check It comes back as the same weapon
check The other racks keep theirs too
expect 1 log (RANDOMIZE every game, round 0)

test classic-holo
title CLASSIC + HOLOGRAMS: a plain weapon's ghost
fresh
setup option weapon_spawns classic_holograms
setup goto -3.35 3.07 -2.75 facing 120
setup spawner wait 40
check The weapon's ghost lies on the floor here
check It fills; at 0:00 the plain weapon lies in it
expect 1 log look classic + holograms)

test classic
title CLASSIC: plain weapons, a fixed timer, no ghost
fresh
setup option weapon_spawns classic
setup goto -7.6 7.6 -2.75 facing 315
check Take the weapon: no ghost on the empty spot
check A plain weapon comes back on its timer
check Walk over a weapon you hold: ammo refills
expect 1 log look classic)

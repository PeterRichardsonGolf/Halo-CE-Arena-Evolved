# 1.0 rc5 WEAPON SPAWNERS: GAME OPTIONS > WEAPONS > WEAPON SPAWNS (shown while AE WEAPONS is on) picks RACKS, the
# spawners' holograms with each spot on its set's logic, or CLASSIC: the same spots with plain weapons on the ground,
# each on a fixed timer, one weapon a spot, no holograms. Two weapons the map lays together are one spot (Wizard's two
# pistols by its north-west platform). A map's layout may carry a comp section, AE's comp positions, played under the
# COMP set alone (Prisoner H1PB: battle rifles on the tower tops, combat ARs below, the shotgun low, the rockets mid).
# Logs: "look racks)" / "look classic)" on the spawners' line, "merges item 5", "comp section: 5 items".
list spawners-rc5
revision 1
title WEAPON SPAWNERS: RACKS, CLASSIC, COMP

group wizard
map h1pb_wizard
gametype AE TEAM SLY
set debug.weapon_set casual
set bots.count 0

test merged-spot
title Wizard: the two pistols lie as one spot
setup goto -7.6 7.6 -2.75 facing 315
check One weapon lies where the map put two pistols
check Take it: one ghost fills on that spot
expect 1 log merges item 5

test racks
title RACKS: a taken weapon's ghost fills as it returns
setup goto -7.6 7.6 -2.75 facing 315
setup spawner wait 40
check The weapon's outlined ghost lies on its spot
check It fills; at 0:00 the weapon lies in it
expect 1 log look racks)

test classic
title CLASSIC: plain weapons, a fixed timer, no ghost
fresh
setup option weapon_spawns classic
setup goto -7.6 7.6 -2.75 facing 315
check Take the weapon: no ghost on the empty spot
check A plain weapon comes back on its timer
check Walk over a weapon you hold: ammo refills
expect 1 log look classic)

group prisoner
map h1pb_prisoner
gametype AE COMP TS
set debug.weapon_set comp
set bots.count 0

test comp-layout
title Prisoner COMP: AE's comp positions, CLASSIC
setup option weapon_spawns classic
setup goto 7.5 4.0 5.59 facing 45
check Battle rifles on both tower tops, rockets mid
check Combat ARs on the walkways, shotgun low
check No sniper and no plasma weapon anywhere
expect 1 log comp section: 5 items only under

# The Theater's camera bar: a slim panel above the playback bar names what the sticks and their clicks do in the view being watched,
# in Content Evolved's words (BEHIND: ZOOM, ORBIT, RESET; FREE CAMERA: MOVE, AIM, TRACK or FREE, FOLLOW PLAYER; CINEMATIC: DISTANCE,
# RESET; EYES: no bar). On the keyboard and mouse it names WASD, the mouse and DOWN instead. The first group records one game with the
# bots (`replay keep` keeps it however short); the watch tests play it back with `replay play newest` and open on their view.
list rc9-smalls
revision 3
title THEATER BAR: THE CAMERA HINTS

group record
map bloodgulch
set network.record_replays true

test record-game
title BLOOD GULCH: play 40 seconds with the bots, then pass
setup bots 7 even normal
setup replay keep
setup replay status
check Play 40 seconds, then pass (a short game is kept too)
expect 1 cmd replay status ok

group watch
map chillout
set network.record_replays false

test hint-behind
retest 2
title REPLAY BAR: BEHIND
setup replay play newest
setup replay seek 15
setup replay behind
check A panel over the bar: LEFT STICK ZOOM, RIGHT STICK ORBIT
check Then RS / DOWN RESET (R3 on a PlayStation pad)
check On the keyboard: W / S ZOOM, MOUSE ORBIT, DOWN RESET
expect 1 cmd replay behind ok

test hint-free
retest 3
title REPLAY BAR: FREE CAMERA
setup replay play newest
setup replay seek 15
setup replay free
check LEFT STICK MOVE, RIGHT STICK AIM, RS / DOWN FREE
check It tracks the player first; RS click: FREE becomes TRACK
check Keyboard: WASD MOVE, MOUSE AIM, DOWN, no L3 hint
expect 1 cmd replay free ok

test hint-cinematic-eyes
retest 2
title REPLAY BAR: CINEMATIC, EYES, PAUSE
setup replay play newest
setup replay seek 15
setup replay cinematic
check LEFT STICK DISTANCE, then LS RESET; nothing overlaps
check Y to the eyes: no camera panel; Start: no panel
check Narrow the window to 4:3: the panel stays in the frame
expect 1 cmd replay cinematic ok

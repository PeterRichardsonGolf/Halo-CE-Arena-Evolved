# FIRING RANGE (Content Evolved's, firing_range.c): a level written in code on Blood Gulch's map: a floor and walls, a mirror,
# an arena to the north, a bench holding each weapon of the Tag Injector profile ae_dev (the one DEV MODE's `inject` fills) with a
# dummy past its end, and a stand of each AI to the east. The range opens from the menus with DEV MODE's `range [<profile>]`;
# each test takes a few weapons and AI of a Custom Edition map into the profile first, then opens the range.
# You start in the middle of the room, facing the arena (north): the benches are to your left, the AI stands to your right.
list firing-range
revision 3
title FIRING RANGE: THE LEVEL IN CODE

test benches
title BENCHES: the weapon lies on its bench
check The bench to your left holds the weapon
check Walk to it: it is yours to pick up
menus
setup inject open h2_warlock
setup inject take 0 0
setup range
expect 1 log firing range: 1 weapons laid out

test dummy
title DUMMY: shoot it, the time to kill is told
check A dummy stands past the end of the bench, facing you
check Shoot it dead: DUMMY: killed in ... s shows
menus
setup inject open h2_warlock
setup inject take 0 0
setup range
expect 1 log firing range: 1 weapons laid out

test arena
title ARENA: the AI stand to the east
check The arena's low walls and door are ahead of you
check One AI stands to your right; walk to it, press action
menus
setup inject open h2_warlock
setup inject take 1 0
setup inject take 1 1
setup range
expect 1 log and 2 AI stood

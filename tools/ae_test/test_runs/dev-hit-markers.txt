# DEV MODE's hit markers: brackets round the reticle when your damage lands (red on a kill).
list dev-hit-markers
revision 1
title DEV MODE HIT MARKERS

test hit-white
title A hit shows white brackets
check Shoot a bot: white corner brackets round the reticle
check They hold a moment and fade, and a new hit restarts them

# SKIP TRAINING (game.campaign_skip_training, off by default; DEV MODE `skip_training status|on|off`): the first campaign level
# (Pillar of Autumn) opens with a cryo wake-up, a look calibration with prompts and the walk to the bridge when it is played alone
# on normal. With the setting on, alone on normal the level takes the short start its own script uses in co-op and on heroic and up:
# the cryo exit, then play, with no calibration prompts and no first-contact scene. The setting off plays the training as before
# (it holds the player's controls off for a minute, so no staged test waits for it).
list skip-training
revision 3
title SKIP TRAINING

group on
map a10
gametype coop
set game.campaign_skip_training true

test skip-on
title Setting on: the training is skipped
check You stand free after the cryo exit, with control
check No LOOK AT THE LIGHTS prompt, no calibration choice
check Walk, look and fire: all work at once
expect 1 log skip training: mission_a10 is told heroic

test skip-squad
title With the AI squad: both stand with you
setup companion 2
check After the cryo exit two Spartans stand beside you
check They follow when you walk on

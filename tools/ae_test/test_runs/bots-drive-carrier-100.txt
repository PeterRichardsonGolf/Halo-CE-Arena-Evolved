# A BOT DRIVES THE FLAG HOME: a teammate bot at the wheel of a vehicle the flag's carrier rides in drives to the
# carrier's own stand, where the flag scores. It does not stop for crew or honk for a gunner while the carrier rides.
# Log with bot events: "bots: ev=drive_carrier bot=N ... goal=stand d=D" every 5 s while it drives you. The scene runs
# the built-in CTF: its flag is taken by touch.
list bots-drive-carrier-100
revision 2
title A BOT DRIVES THE FLAG HOME

group dangercanyon
map dangercanyon@mcc
gametype CTF
set bots.count 7
set debug.bot_events true

test flag-ride
title You hold their flag; a bot drives you home
setup unseen me
setup goto flag_theirs
setup spawn mp_warthog here
setup seat driver mate
setup seat passenger
check You hold their flag in the passenger seat
check The bot drives you off toward your base
check It stops at your flag; you score
expect 1 cmd unseen me ok
expect 1 log ev=flag_pickup
expect 2 log ev=drive_carrier

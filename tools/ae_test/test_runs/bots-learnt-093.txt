# 0.9.3 BOTS: bots keep what they learnt of a map (bots.learn_map, on). Where drivers wedged and where bots on foot got
# stuck is kept from game to game in the config folder's bots_learnt/<map>.txt, and their routes pay more there from the
# next game on (never a wall). A jump you land that rises or drops 0.8 units or more, and that you live 5 s after, is a
# link the bots may take from the next map load (only when it beats walking; a bot that sees you is fighting and
# takes no short cut that saves under 30 units, so hide from them). The log says "bots: learnt: jump ..." when
# yours is kept, and "bots: learnt: ... written" at the game's end.
list bots-learnt-093
revision 5
title BOTS: THE MAP LEARNT FROM GAME TO GAME

group temple
map temple
gametype AE BR TS
set bots.count 5
set bots.teams even
set bots.skill elite
set debug.bot_events true
set bots.learn_map true

test your-jump
title TEMPLE: a jump of yours is kept
check Jump up onto a ledge you would walk round to reach
check Stay alive 5 s: the log says your jump is kept
expect 2 log bots: learnt: jump

test bots-take-it
title TEMPLE: the bots take your jump after a reload
fresh
check Hide on top of your jump, out of the bots' sight
check A bot hunting you comes up your jump
expect 1 log jumps of yours are links

test off-switch
title TEMPLE: the switch off
setup config bots.learn_map off
check Jump again and live: no "learnt" line in the log

AE COMBAT AR (weapon pack ae_ar_modern) - credits

The rifle's model, first-person animations, textures, shaders, effects and sounds are the work of CMT (the Custom
Mapping Team), from the stock-compatible source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4 (the Evolved team:
Teh Lag, Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names): CMT's individual combat weapon system
rifle over CMT's assault rifle model. They are used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things
you didn't make") and are unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs: the models converted, the multipurpose maps'
channels reordered, the Ogg Vorbis sounds encoded again as Xbox ADPCM, the high-resolution HUD made the Xbox's.

Made for Arena Evolved (our own work, drawn or computed by the scripts in tools/weapon_pack/):
- the ammunition counter (2 rows of 18 rounds in groups of 6) and its low-ammo flash;
- the aiming reticle, drawn from geometry in the SMG pack's form;
- the ammunition panel's layout: its positions, sizes and references are numbers set by AE, in the structure of
  the stock assault rifle's HUD (no art of the game's);
- the weapon's numbers (rate of fire, magazine, error, zoom, aim assist, bullet speed and range) and the damage of a
  round, set by AE.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the rifle needs of the game (the stock HUD's numbers
and frames, impact materials, the held weapon's collision and melee) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

The pickup prompt's and kill feed's picture is CMT's own, from CMT's pickup icon sheet (hudv4.1): its assault rifle's.

Kind: community model. CMT's attribution note in the release says its models, textures and shaders are CMT's own
and that "the only non-custom assets in this release (i.e., from Halo games other than CE) are certain music and sfx
files": the rifle's model, animations and textures are CMT's. Which sounds those are the note does not say.

Thank you, CMT.

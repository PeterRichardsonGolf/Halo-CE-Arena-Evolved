AE BATTLE RIFLE (weapon pack ae_battle_rifle) - credits [DRAFT: Task 19 settles the final text]

The rifle's model, textures, shaders, first-person animations, effects and sounds are the work of CMT (the Custom
Mapping Team), from the source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the Evolved team: Teh Lag,
Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names). Sound design: Beaucephal and Xalener.
They are used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and are
unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs: Open Sauce's extension blocks removed, the multipurpose maps' channels
reordered, the Ogg Vorbis sounds encoded again as Xbox ADPCM, the models converted.

Made for Arena Evolved (our own work, drawn or computed by the scripts in tools/weapon_pack/):
- the lighting values and the brightened base map;
- the gun screen's digits and compass;
- the sharp scope: CMT's scope lines and ring redrawn four times as large, with no zoom label;
- the ammunition counter (2 rows of 18 rounds in groups of 3) and its low-ammo flash;
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture of the rifle, drawn from the rifle's model;
- the ammunition panel's layout: its positions, sizes and references are numbers set by AE, in the structure of
  the stock assault rifle's HUD (no art of the game's);
- the burst behaviour, which is code in the game and no part of this pack.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the rifle needs of the game (the stock HUD's
numbers and frames, a reflection map, impact materials) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

The aiming reticle is our own drawing (br_reticle.py): a ring and four ticks, which spread a little with each
burst.

Thank you, CMT.

AE SIDEARM (weapon pack ae_sidearm) - credits

The sidearm's models (the slide magnum, third-person and first-person), shaders, textures, first-person and
third-person animations, collision, sounds and HUD (its ammunition meter, reticle and picture of the magnum) are
ZTeam's, from the ZTeam tag pack (released on halomaps.org). The pack names no individual authors, so none are named
here. They are used as released, but for what an Xbox map needs: the models converted to the Xbox model format, the
multipurpose maps' channels reordered.

The aiming reticles (the unzoomed one and the zoomed one) are CMT's, from the pistol HUD of the stock-compatible release
of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved's source (the reticle sheet unchanged; the Xbox-map scale CMT's HUD needs applied).
Thank you, CMT.

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the models have the trigger guard cut off (the faces of the guard dropped from the model's source and the models
  compiled again);
- the body textures are ZTeam's own, desaturated and made jet black, with the rear sight's face cleaned and the front
  post and two rear dots in green; the masks that place the green and the clean face are ours; the shaders are
  ZTeam's with a lower brightness;
- the weapon's numbers (magazine, rate of fire, error, zoom) and the damage of a round, set to our own values;
- the round and the firing effect are the stock pistol's with our damage and ZTeam's shot sound;
- the HUD keeps ZTeam's ammunition meter and picture of the magnum, with CMT's reticles in place of ZTeam's; ZTeam's flashlight left out; the HUD's rounds panel is the map's stock one (ZTeam's chain of panels ends in a
  full-screen visor frame);
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture is ZTeam's own picture of the magnum, resampled onto a sprite sheet (nothing
  drawn);
- the firing kick is the stock pistol's with a rumble.

The pack's round and firing effect are built from the stock pistol's tags (no pixel, sound or model of the game's):
what else the sidearm needs of the game (the stock rounds panel, ammunition pickup, melee, impacts, materials,
muzzle flash) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Kind: unknown. ZTeam's models are made for Custom Edition (their own nodes, a Custom Edition polygon budget) and
recreate a later game's sidearm; nothing in the release says whether they and the sounds were modelled and
recorded by ZTeam or taken from that game, so this pack is not yet classed as a community model or a direct port.

Thank you, ZTeam.

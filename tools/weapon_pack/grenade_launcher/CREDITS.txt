AE GRENADE LAUNCHER (weapon pack ae_grenade_launcher) - credits

The grenade launcher's models (third-person and first-person), its grenade's model, shaders, textures (the gun's
screen too), first-person and third-person animations, collision, sounds, the grenade's effects and its HUD (the
grenade meter and the reticle) are ZTeam's, from the ZTeam tag pack (released on halomaps.org):
the pack's grenade_launcher/hr weapon, with the first-person animations and ready sound of its hr_cad folder, and
the HUD ZTeam gave it (ZTeam's brute shot HUD). The pack names no individual authors, so none are named here. They
are used as released, but for what an Xbox map needs: the models converted to the Xbox model format, the
multipurpose map's channels reordered, and the explosion's fire flash drawn with its own sprites (ZTeam's set it
to take sprites its bitmap does not have). The identifier painted beside the arming arrows of the gun's screen is
cleared (one rectangle made transparent; every other pixel ZTeam's).

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the weapon's and the grenade's numbers (6 a magazine, 12 to start and 24 at most, rate of fire, aim assist,
  the grenade's speed, drop and fuse, the burst's damage and radius), set to our own values;
- hold the trigger to keep the grenade from bursting, let go to burst it (the trigger tracks its grenade);
- ZTeam's second trigger (a charged EMP grenade) left out, and the first trigger's charge with it;
- the grenade bursts on its fuse, on a player or when the trigger is let go: the damage ZTeam's bounce made (which
  set the grenade off at its first bounce) left out, and the grenade has no collision model (nothing can hit it or
  shoot it down);
- the HUD's rounds panel is the map's stock one (ZTeam's chain of panels ends in a full-screen visor frame);
- a rumble added to ZTeam's firing kick;
- materials and melee are the map's stock assault rifle's.

No picture of a grenade launcher is used: none of the releases we draw on has one, so the weapon's name is
written where a picture would be (the swap and pickup prompts, the kill feed and the HUD).

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the launcher needs of the game (materials, melee,
the stock rounds panel, a noise map) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Kind: unknown. ZTeam's models are made for Custom Edition and recreate a later game's grenade launcher; nothing in
the release says whether they and the sounds were modelled and recorded by ZTeam or taken from that game, so this
pack is not yet classed as a community model or a direct port.

Thank you, ZTeam.

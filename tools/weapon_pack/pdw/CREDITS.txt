AE BURST PDW (weapon pack ae_pdw) - credits

The burst PDW's models (ZTeam's compact submachine gun, third-person and first-person), shaders, textures (the
alternate "h3b" texture set the pack wears), first-person animations, collision, sounds and HUD (its ammunition
meter and picture of the weapon) are ZTeam's, from the ZTeam tag pack (released on halomaps.org). The pack names no
individual authors, so none are named here. They are used as released, but for what an Xbox map needs: the models
converted to the Xbox model format, the multipurpose map's channels reordered.

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the weapon's numbers (magazine, burst, rate of fire, error, aim assist) and the damage and speed of a round, set to
  our own values;
- the round and the firing effect are the stock assault rifle's with our numbers and ZTeam's shot sound;
- the models wear ZTeam's h3b texture set (its two shaders in place of the h3 ones); ZTeam's third-person reload
  effect is left out;
- the HUD's rounds panel is the map's stock one (ZTeam's chain of panels ends in a full-screen visor frame), and its
  ammunition meter shows ZTeam's own 36-round art from the same sheet;
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture is ZTeam's own picture of the weapon, resampled onto a sprite sheet (nothing
  drawn);
- the firing kick is the stock assault rifle's with a rumble.

The aiming reticle is CMT's, from the SMG HUD of the stock-compatible release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved's
source (the reticle sheet unchanged; the Xbox-map scale CMT's HUD needs applied). ZTeam's reticle is not used.
Thank you, CMT.

The pack's round and firing effect are built from the stock assault rifle's tags (no pixel, sound or model of the
game's): what else the PDW needs of the game (the stock rounds panel, ammunition pickup, melee, impacts, materials,
muzzle flash) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Kind: unknown. ZTeam's models are made for Custom Edition (their own nodes, a Custom Edition polygon budget) and
recreate a later game's submachine gun; nothing in the release says whether they and the sounds were modelled and
recorded by ZTeam or taken from that game, so this pack is not yet classed as a community model or a direct port.

Thank you, ZTeam.

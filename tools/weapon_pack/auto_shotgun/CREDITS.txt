AE AUTO SHOTGUN (weapon pack ae_auto_shotgun) - credits

The auto-shotgun's model (the modern combat shotgun, worn in first and third person), shaders, textures, first-person
animations, collision, sounds (the shot, the draw's rack, the shell-by-shell reload, the forend stroke at the end of a reload, melee) and
HUD (its 6-shell meter, round reticle and picture of the shotgun) are ZTeam's, from the ZTeam tag pack (released on
halomaps.org). The pack names no individual authors, so none are named here. They are used as released, but for what an
Xbox map needs: the model converted to the Xbox model format, the multipurpose map's channels reordered.

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the first-person animations made semi-automatic from ZTeam's (tools/weapon_pack/auto_shotgun_anims.py, our own
  script): a shot no longer works the forend (the recoil is ZTeam's, played faster, with the loading port's cycle and
  the spent shell moved into it), the draw racks it once (ZTeam's own draw and its sound), the reload's enter and
  shell loop play about a fifth faster, the idle posing is ZTeam's one that leaves the forend alone, and the forend is
  worked once, at the end of each reload, by ZTeam's own exit animation; the stroke sounds of the shot and the
  forend-check posing are left out;
- the third-person animation graph (a forend stroke) and ZTeam's flashlight are left out;
- the weapon's numbers (shells, rate of fire, pellets, spread, aim assist) and the damage, falloff and range of a
  pellet, set to our own values;
- the pellet and the firing effect are the stock shotgun's with our numbers and ZTeam's shot sound;
- the HUD's rounds panel is the map's stock one (ZTeam's chain of panels ends in a full-screen visor frame);
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture is ZTeam's own picture of the shotgun, resampled onto a sprite sheet (nothing
  drawn);
- the firing kick is the stock shotgun's with our rumble.

The pack's pellet and firing effect are built from the stock shotgun's tags (no pixel, sound or model of the game's):
what else the shotgun needs of the game (the stock rounds panel, ammunition pickup, melee, impacts, materials, muzzle
flash, empty click) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Kind: unknown. ZTeam's models are made for Custom Edition (their own nodes, a Custom Edition polygon budget) and
recreate a later game's shotgun; nothing in the release says whether they and the sounds were modelled and recorded by
ZTeam or taken from that game, so this pack is not yet classed as a community model or a direct port.

Thank you, ZTeam.

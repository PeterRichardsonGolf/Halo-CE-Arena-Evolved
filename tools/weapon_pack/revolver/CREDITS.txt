AE HEAVY REVOLVER (weapon pack ae_revolver) - credits

The heavy revolver's models (the h3b world and first-person models), its weapon tag, first-person and third-person
animations, collision, sounds, metal shader (its cube map and detail map), textures and HUD (its ammunition meter,
aiming reticle and picture of the revolver) are ZTeam's, from the ZTeam tag pack (released on halomaps.org). The
pack names no individual authors, so none are named here. ZTeam left the h3b models untextured; this pack dresses
them with ZTeam's own textures of the h3 version of the same gun. They are used as released, but for what an Xbox
map needs: the models converted to the Xbox model format, the multipurpose map's channels reordered.

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the textures re-dressed for the h3b models (revolver_art.py, nothing painted by hand): ZTeam's lights carried to
  their places on the gun, the drum and the receiver's side panel a dark red and the rest a dark metal (each from
  ZTeam's own brightness, so its panel lines and wear stay), ZTeam's lights in a red;
- the weapon's numbers (one slug a shot, drum, rate of fire, error, aim assist) and the damage of a slug and of a
  blow, set to our own values; the HUD's ammunition meter ZTeam's eight-shell one (from the same sheets) and its
  reticle drawn round;
- the slug, the firing effect and the melee are the stock pistol's with our numbers and ZTeam's shot sound;
- ZTeam's light left out; the HUD's rounds panel is the map's stock one (ZTeam's chain of panels ends in a
  full-screen visor frame);
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture is ZTeam's own picture of the revolver, resampled onto a sprite sheet
  (nothing drawn);
- the firing kick is the stock pistol's with a rumble.
- the first-person animations are ZTeam's with the whole view (arms and gun) set back a little, so the gun
  sits off and lower right at AE's field of view.

The pack's slug, firing effect, kick and melee are built from the stock pistol's tags (no pixel, sound or model of
the game's): what else the revolver needs of the game (the stock rounds panel, ammunition pickup, melee response,
impacts, materials, muzzle flash) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Kind: unknown. ZTeam's models are made for Custom Edition (their own nodes, a Custom Edition polygon budget) and
recreate a later game's weapon; nothing in the release says whether they and the sounds were modelled and recorded
by ZTeam or taken from that game, so this pack is not yet classed as a community model or a direct port.

Thank you, ZTeam.

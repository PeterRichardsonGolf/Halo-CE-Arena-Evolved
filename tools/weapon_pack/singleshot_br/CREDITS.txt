AE SINGLESHOT BR (weapon pack ae_singleshot_br) - credits

The rifle's effects, sounds, scope lines and HUD art are the work of CMT (the Custom Mapping Team), from the source
release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the
contributors their credits.txt names). Sound design: Beaucephal and Xalener. They are used under the release's own
terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and are unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs: Open
Sauce's extension blocks removed, the multipurpose maps' channels reordered, the Ogg Vorbis sounds encoded again as
Xbox ADPCM.

Made for Arena Evolved (our own work, drawn or computed by the scripts in tools/weapon_pack/):
- the lighting values and the brightened base map;
- the gun screen's digits and compass;
- the sharp scope: CMT's scope lines and ring redrawn four times as large, with no zoom label;
- the ammunition counter (2 rows of 18 rounds in groups of 3) and its low-ammo flash;
- the ammunition panel's layout: its positions, sizes and references are numbers set by AE, in the structure of
  the stock assault rifle's HUD (no art of the game's);
- the rifle's numbers (one round a pull), which are code in the game and no part of this pack.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the rifle needs of the game (the stock HUD's
numbers and frames, a reflection map, impact materials) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

The pickup prompt's and kill feed's picture is CMT's own, from CMT's pickup icon sheet (hudv4.1): its battle rifle's.

The aiming reticle is our own drawing (br_reticle.py): a ring and four ticks, the battle rifle pack's own.

The rifle's look (its third-person and first-person models, their shaders and textures, and its first-person
animations) is the battle rifle of CE+ X, the work of the CE+ X team, as the CE Digsite Multiplayer team's maps carry
it. It is used as it is, but for its first-person animations, which play this pack's own melee, ready and reload
sounds. Its HUD, reticle and sounds are the AE battle rifle's.

Thank you, CMT, the CE+ X team and the CE Digsite Multiplayer team.

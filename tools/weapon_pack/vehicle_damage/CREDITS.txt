AE VEHICLE DAMAGE (pack ae_vehicle_damage) - credits

The vehicles' damage and death effects (sparks, smoke, fire, the glass breaking, the hood and the wings coming off,
the explosions), the burning wrecks of the warthog and the banshee, and their particles, textures and sounds are
the work of CMT (the Custom Mapping Team), from the source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the
Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names). Sound design:
Beaucephal and Xalener. They are used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things you
didn't make") and are unchanged but for what an Xbox map and a network game need: Open Sauce's extension blocks
removed, the multipurpose maps' channels reordered, the Ogg Vorbis sounds encoded again as Xbox ADPCM, the models
converted; the parts that name campaign tags no multiplayer map has (underwater bubbles, panel debris, the
damaged-engine loop sound) and the warthog's thrown wheel (a vehicle) left out.

Made for Arena Evolved: when the effects play and the wrecks stand in (port/linux/game/ae_vehicle_pack.c), under
the gametype rule VEHICLES DESTRUCTIBLE.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the effects need of the game (stock particles,
bitmaps and sounds every multiplayer map has) it names, and the map the player owns provides. The map's own
vehicles keep their own models.

Thank you, CMT.

AE SMG (weapon pack ae_smg) - credits

The SMG's model, textures, shaders, first-person animations, effects and sounds are the work of CMT (the Custom
Mapping Team), from the source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the Evolved team: Teh Lag,
Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names). Sound design: Beaucephal and Xalener.
They are used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and are
unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs: Open Sauce's extension blocks removed, the multipurpose maps' channels
reordered, the Ogg Vorbis sounds encoded again as Xbox ADPCM, the models converted.

Made for Arena Evolved (our own work, drawn or computed by the scripts in tools/weapon_pack/):
- the lighting values and the brightened base map;
- the aiming reticle, drawn from geometry in the form of a plain SMG reticle;
- the ammunition counter (3 rows of 20 rounds in groups of 5) and its low-ammo flash;
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture of the SMG, drawn from its model;
- the ammunition panel's layout: its positions, sizes and references are numbers set by AE, in the structure of
  the stock assault rifle's HUD (no art of the game's);
- the weapon's numbers (rate of fire, magazine, error, aim assist, bullet speed and range), set to tuned values;
- the damage of a round, set by AE.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the SMG needs of the game (the stock HUD's
numbers and frames, reflection maps, impact materials, the held weapon's collision) it names, and the map the
player owns provides.

Thank you, CMT.

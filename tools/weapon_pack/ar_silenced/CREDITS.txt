AE SILENCED AR (weapon pack ae_ar_silenced) - credits

The silenced assault rifle (the "ma5s": its silencer's model and textures, its firing sound and firing effect) is
the work of the makers of the Halo 2 Mappack Collection v2, from its Gemini (h2d_gemini; boardingaction.map in the
collection): Dominater01, S3anyboy and GoodFellaPro, with help from Team NHE (the collection's readme: "This Was A
Collaberted Effort By Dominater01, S3anyboy and GoodFellaPro With Help Of Team NHE"). Its body is the stock
assault rifle's: its models hold the stock assault rifle's model with the silencer added, as the collection made
them.

Made for Arena Evolved: the casing now leaves the ejection port on the side (the firing effect's casing made at
the 'primary ejection' marker, as the stock assault rifle's, not at the silencer's tip). Nothing else is changed:
the weapon's numbers are the collection's (the stock assault rifle's, silent to the AI's hearing, one 2x zoom).

What the weapon needs of the game (the stock assault rifle's HUD, animations, textures, bullet, casing, impacts,
melee and collision) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Thank you, Dominater01, S3anyboy, GoodFellaPro and Team NHE.

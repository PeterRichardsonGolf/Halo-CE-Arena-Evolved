AE SND BOMB (pack ae_bomb) - credits

The bomb's blast (the fireball, the smoke, the sparks, the flash and the explosion sounds) is the work of CMT (the
Custom Mapping Team), from the source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the Evolved team: Teh Lag,
Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names). Sound design: Beaucephal and Xalener. It is
used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and is unchanged but for
what an Xbox map and a network game need: Open Sauce's extension blocks removed, the parts that deal damage or name
campaign tags no multiplayer map has (underwater parts, a thrown wheel, a garbage object) left out. The game deals the
blast's damage itself.

The timer on the bomb's face is drawn from the Halo Custom Edition Editing Kit's Digsite grenade timer art (Digsite,
officially sanctioned content for the community). The rest of the bomb's surfaces are baked, when the pack is built,
from textures of the game itself (the rocket ammo case, the warthog, the a10 tech rack, Blood Gulch's metal and
ground, the grenades' plastic and the lens flare) and from CMT's textures: the pack's textures and shaders are
stock-derived, and the pack holds them because Arena Evolved requires the base game. The model's geometry, the
timer's bars and beacon, the multipurpose maps and the pickup chirp (a high quick chirp-chirp, synthesized) are our
own.

Made for Arena Evolved: the Search and Destroy bomb's look, timer and blast (port/linux/game/ae_bomb_pack.c).

Thank you, CMT.

AE SMOKE GRENADE (pack ae_smoke) - credits

The smoke cloud's sprites, their motion and their bitmap are the game's own stock smoke effect, particle and point
physics tags (Bungie's), which every multiplayer map has: the pack holds our numbers for them (how many, how big, how
long, how they drift) and names the map's bitmap. It holds no picture, sound or model of the game's.

Made for Arena Evolved: the cloud the AE smoke grenade throws under SEARCH AND DESTROY (port/linux/game/ae_smoke_grenade.c).
What blocks the bots' sight is the game's smoke volume, not these sprites (ae_smoke_volume.c).

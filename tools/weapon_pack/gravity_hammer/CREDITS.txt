AE GRAVITY HAMMER (weapon pack ae_gravity_hammer) - credits

Gravity hammer from Grifball Beta (grifball.map) by SlappyThePirate, 2011; the hammer art's original maker is not
named.

The hammer (its model and first-person model, animations, textures, sounds, effects and particles, its HUD, reticle
and weapon icon sheet) is taken as it is from that Custom Edition map's tags. Nothing of its art is changed: only the
tags' paths are the pack's own (gravity_hammer\hammer\...), the models, one bitmap, one sound and the two HUDs are
converted to what an Xbox map holds, and the picture the pickup prompts show is the map's own picture of the hammer,
resampled. Arena Evolved sets the swing's numbers in the classic way, one blow one kill: a blast of 255 that kills a
full shield and health within 1.8 units of the hammer's head, strips the shield to 2.2 and falls to nothing at 2.6, a
reach of 2.8 units, one swing every 1.43 seconds; the melee button is a blow of the hammer too (175 to the one it lands
on).

Kind: community model. Shipped as is, to every player.

What the hammer needs of the game (the rocket launcher's melee response, the stock material effects and impact sounds) it
names, and the map the player owns provides.

Thank you, SlappyThePirate. If you made the hammer's art, or know who did, and want it credited differently or taken
out, tell us.

AE BOLT SNIPER (weapon pack ae_sniper_bolt) - credits

The bolt-action sniper rifle is the CE+ team's (VKMT), from CE+ X 1.01, the CE+ project's Xbox release: its
weapon, its first-person model and animations, its trigger and its HUD (the dw2 folders), and the third-person
model with its shaders and textures, which CE+ X carries from Digsite (the Digsite Crew's work, the digsite
folders). They are taken from CE+ X's own multiplayer map as the disc has them. One change: four empty elements of
its HUD, which the game never draws (its tag check reports each "has no bitmap: not drawn"), are dropped; and the
scope crosshairs' offsets (which CE+ X laid out twice as wide) are the stock sniper rifle's, so they draw at its size. The
HUD otherwise looks the same.

CE+ project credits (2019-2021), as the team gives them: project leads Sean T (lead, tagging, Xbox porting, design
and balancing) and Ludus (lead, 3D art); 3D art Ludus, Omargabagu, Killzone64, PopeTX28; AI (animation, tagging,
scripting); Vengeful Vadam (design, dev support); tjsase / TJ Sauce (HD upscales); xScruffyDaSasquatchx (MP
balancing); Holy Crust (UI/HUD); DDS; Shelly (scripting); Vuthakral; savinpvtmike (rigging, skinning); Roushyy
(animation); Henskelion; Elote; Neo Te Aika. Additional support: Kavawuvi (Invader), R93 Sniper, ConnorDawn, Rosy
Muscovy, Booey (Odyssey Sound Team), MrChromed, Maik, Spiral, Galaxy, aLTis, SBB Crew, Berthalamew, Aegis Wolf,
Digsite Crew. Special thanks: the TSCE team and the Odyssey Sound Team.

The weapon's numbers are CE+ X's (one round every 1.25 s, 4 a magazine, 12 to start and 24 at most, zoom 2x and
8x); its bullet, damage, firing effect, sounds and melee are the stock sniper rifle's, which it names.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the rifle needs of the game (the stock sniper
rifle's bullet, firing effect, sounds, melee, ammunition pickup and scope pictures, the HUD's numbers and frames)
it names, and the map the player owns provides.

The pickup prompt's and kill feed's picture of the rifle is CE+'s own, from CE+ X's pickup icon sheet.

Kind: community model. The rifle's weapon, first-person model, animations and HUD are the CE+ team's own (CE+'s
dw2 folders); the third-person model is Digsite's (the Digsite Crew's release of early official material, which
CE+ X carries in its digsite folders). Nothing of it is taken from another Halo game.

Thank you, VKMT, the CE+ team and the Digsite Crew.

AE TACTICAL SMG (weapon pack ae_smg_tactical) - credits

The tactical SMG's model, textures, shaders, first-person arms and animations, effects, sounds and zoom lines are the
work of CMT (the Custom Mapping Team), from the source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the
Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names). Sound design: Beaucephal
and Xalener. They are used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and
are unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs: Open Sauce's extension blocks removed, the multipurpose maps' channels
reordered, the Ogg Vorbis sounds encoded again as Xbox ADPCM, the models converted. CMT's model carries its
first-person arms and two flashlight cones: the third-person model has the arms and the cones cut out, the
first-person model the cones cut out. The zoom lines are CMT's with the magnification label and the small print
left out, and the zoom mask is carried out to a wider picture.

Made for Arena Evolved (our own work, drawn or computed by the scripts in tools/weapon_pack/):
- the lighting values and the brightened base map;
- the aiming reticle, drawn from geometry in the form of a plain SMG reticle;
- the ammunition counter (3 rows of 20 rounds in groups of 5) and its low-ammo flash;
- the ammunition panel's layout: its positions, sizes and references are numbers set by AE, in the structure of
  the stock assault rifle's HUD (no art of the game's);
- the weapon's numbers (rate of fire, magazine, error, zoom, aim assist, bullet speed and range), set to values
  that are the first set and will be tuned in play;
- the damage of a round, set by AE.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the tactical SMG needs of the game (the stock HUD's
numbers and frames, reflection maps, impact materials, the held weapon's collision) it names, and the map the
player owns provides.

The pickup prompt's and kill feed's picture is CMT's own, from CMT's pickup icon sheet (hudv4.1).

Thank you, CMT.

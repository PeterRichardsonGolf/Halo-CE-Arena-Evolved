AE RAVAGER (weapon pack ae_ravager) - credits

The ravager's model, first-person model and animations, shaders, HUD, effects and sounds, and its weapon, bolt and
damage, are the work of CMT (the Custom Mapping Team), from the stock-compatible source release of The Silent
Cartographer: Evolved 1.4 (the Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt
names): CMT's prototype ravager (evolved_prototypes/ravager), which uses CMT's spiker model and animations, and the charge
glow, burst and burn effects of CMT's prototype devourer (evolved_prototypes/devourer). They are
used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and are unchanged but for
what an Xbox map needs (the models converted, the multipurpose maps' channels reordered, the high-resolution HUD made
the Xbox's, a flesh impact sound's Ogg Vorbis samples encoded again as Xbox ADPCM, one shader's detail map, which names
a campaign bitmap no multiplayer map has, left empty) and for its look.

Its look is ours, made from CMT's own textures: the spiker's body and clip pictures recoloured by numbers
(tools/weapon_pack/ravager_art.py, look B3bd: crimson panels, near-black steel trim, a tempered-steel blade from straw
to blue, white-cyan glowing seams and panel lines through the self-illumination, cyan lights),
under the ravager's own copies of CMT's shaders and models, so that the ravager does not look like the spiker. No new
art is drawn; the HUD's weapon picture and the message icon are CMT's own spiker picture (the ravager's model).

The weapon's numbers are AE's own, not CMT's: the latest-generation ravager's setup tuned for CE. Three lobbed
bolts a pull, a pull every 0.77 s, each bolt bursting with a small splash (4 bolts strip a full shield, 7 kill, x2 on
the head once the shield is down); hold to charge (CMT's devourer charge glow) and release for one big bolt whose
burst leaves a burning pool (CMT's devourer burn pulses, our damage); a 1.4x zoom, 6 pulls to overheat, a 3 s vent and
a battery of 36 bolts; a pad pulse with each pull and a heavy one with a charged bolt. The held weapon's collision and
melee are the stock assault rifle's, left to the map.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the ravager needs of the game (impact materials,
decals, particles, sounds and a reflection map) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

The pickup prompt's and kill feed's picture is CMT's own, from CMT's pickup icon sheet (hudv4.1).

Kind: community model. CMT's attribution note in the release says its models, textures and shaders are CMT's own
and that "the only non-custom assets in this release (i.e., from Halo games other than CE) are certain music and sfx
files": the ravager's models, animations and textures are CMT's. Which sounds those are the note does not say.

Thank you, CMT.

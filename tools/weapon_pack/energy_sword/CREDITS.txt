AE ENERGY SWORD (weapon pack ae_energy_sword) - credits

Two authors' work, two views.

The sword itself, as other players see it and as it lies on the ground, is the work of CMT (the Custom Mapping Team),
from the stock-compatible source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4 (the Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi,
Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names): CMT's energy sword, energy_sword of the evolved set: its
model, shaders, textures, light and effects, its weapon and its melee damage. They are used under the release's own
terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and are unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs: the
model converted, the pickup message index the stock one's, the two glow widgets left out (their sprite is a bitmap of
the PC kit that no Xbox map has), the detonation's one sound left out (a boom of the PC kit that no Xbox map has), the
blade shaders' "draw before water" flag cleared (the first-person view draws them), and the weapon's "detonates when
dropped" flag cleared (the game counts a weapon put away on a weapon switch as dropped, which deleted the sword).

What the holder sees in first person is CMT's sword mesh put on ZTeam's rig: ZTeam's first-person sword supplies the
node frames ("frame handle", "frame blades") and the animations (its idle, ready, moving, posing, melee, lunge and
lunge in the air, grenade throws), taken as they are, with ZTeam's melee and posing sounds, its HUD with the sword's
reticle, and ZTeam's own picture of the sword for the pickup prompts and the kill feed (resampled, nothing drawn). The
mesh is moved (a uniform scale of 1.0185 and a shift) so that its grip and blade tip sit where ZTeam's sword has them.
CMT's release holds no first-person sword, so the two views come from different authors: CMT's mesh, ZTeam's rig,
animations, sounds, reticle and picture. ZTeam's sword sounds are in it too (revision 2): the ready sound on draw, the hum (its looping sound) while the sword is held, the
drop sound, the hit sound of a blow (sword_impact_flesh), the melee sounds a lunge plays, and the energy impact (melee_impact_energy)
which the clash of two swords plays a third higher. CMT's dripping sparks off the blade are drawn about half as many and live half as
long. The swing (the right trigger) and the lunge's carry are Arena Evolved's own
code, not either author's.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the sword needs of the game (particles' point
physics, a noise map and the plasma rifle's HUD) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Kind: community model, both sources. CMT's attribution note in the release says its models, textures and shaders are
CMT's own and that "the only non-custom assets in this release (i.e., from Halo games other than CE) are certain music
and sfx files": the sword's model and textures are CMT's. ZTeam's tag pack is a community release; where ZTeam's own
sources of the pieces named here lie is not stated in what we have, so they are taken as ZTeam's.

Thank you, CMT. Thank you, ZTeam.

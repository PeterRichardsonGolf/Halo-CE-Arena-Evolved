AE DMR (weapon pack ae_dmr) - credits

The rifle's model, textures, shaders, effects and sounds are the work of CMT (the Custom Mapping Team), from the
source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and
the contributors their credits.txt names). The first-person animations and reload and ready sounds are CMT's battle
rifle's, which CMT's DMR weapon uses. They are used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for things you didn't make") and are unchanged but for
what an Xbox map needs: Open Sauce's extension blocks removed, the multipurpose maps' channels reordered, the Ogg
Vorbis sounds encoded again as Xbox ADPCM, the models converted.

Made for Arena Evolved (our own work, drawn or computed by the scripts in tools/weapon_pack/):
- the lighting values and the brightened base map;
- the zoom overlay (evo_scope.py, the EVO RIFLE's): a soft dark surround round a wide window, black glass marks and
  the readouts' frames, drawn from geometry; the zoom and the rounds in them are drawn by the game;
- the aiming reticle, a bolder form of Kenney's crosshair 033;
- the weapon's behaviour, which is code in the game and no part of this pack.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the rifle needs of the game (the stock HUD's
numbers and frames, reflection maps, impact materials, the held weapon's collision) it names, and the map the
player owns provides.

The aiming reticle follows crosshair 033 of Kenney's Crosshair Pack (www.kenney.nl), released under Creative
Commons Zero. Thank you, Kenney.

Thank you, CMT.

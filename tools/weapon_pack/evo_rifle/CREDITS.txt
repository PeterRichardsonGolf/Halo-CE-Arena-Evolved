AE EVO RIFLE (weapon pack ae_evo_rifle) - credits

The rifle is CMT's DMR: its model, textures, shaders, effects and sounds are the work of CMT (the Custom Mapping
Team), from the source release of The Silent Cartographer: Evolved 1.4.1c (the Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi,
Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt names). The first-person animations and reload and ready
sounds are CMT's battle rifle's, which CMT's DMR weapon uses. They are used under the release's own terms ("Just
don't take credit for things you didn't make"); the model is changed as the list below says, the rest is unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs:
Open Sauce's extension blocks removed, the multipurpose maps' channels reordered, the Ogg Vorbis sounds encoded
again as Xbox ADPCM, the models converted.

Made for Arena Evolved (our own work, drawn or computed by the scripts in tools/weapon_pack/):
- the model's changes to CMT's DMR: the scope taken off, the barrel and the tube under it 0.030 wu shorter, and the
  holo sight below put on the top rail, made smaller and drawn with the rifle's own texture, with a glass for its
  window;
- the aiming reticle (a ring and a split dot) and the same reticle in the sight's window, drawn by scripts;
- the lighting values and the brightened base map;
- the zoom overlay (evo_scope.py): a soft dark surround round a wide window, black glass marks and the readouts'
  frames, drawn from geometry; the zoom and the rounds in them are drawn by the game;
- the holo sight: the H02 Holo of the Accessory Kit by CuiGames (@GodTrigger0, https://cuigames.itch.io), used
  under the kit's licence (in games; its files are not handed out, raw or changed: the pack holds only the sight as
  the game draws it).
- the weapon's behaviour, which is code in the game and no part of this pack.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the rifle needs of the game (the stock HUD's
numbers and frames, reflection maps, impact materials, the held weapon's collision) it names, and the map the
player owns provides.

Thank you, CMT.

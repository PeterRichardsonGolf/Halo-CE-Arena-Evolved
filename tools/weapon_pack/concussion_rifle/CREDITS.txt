AE CONCUSSION RIFLE (weapon pack ae_concussion_rifle) - credits

The concussion rifle's models (third-person and first-person), shaders, textures, first-person animations,
collision, sounds, its explosive bolt (model, effects, contrail, light, explosion and their particles, decal and
sounds), its firing effect and kick, and its HUD (the six-shell ammunition meter and the launcher reticle) are
ZTeam's, from the ZTeam tag pack (released on halomaps.org). The pack names no individual authors, so none are named
here. They are used as released, but for what an Xbox map needs: the models converted to the Xbox model format, the
multipurpose maps' channels reordered.

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the weapon's numbers (magazine, rate of fire, aim assist), the bolt's speed and drop, and its blast (damage,
  radius, knockback) set to our own values;
- the firing kick and the blast's rumble given our own pad timings;
- the HUD's rounds panel is the map's stock one (ZTeam's chain of panels ends in a full-screen visor frame); its
  reticle is another of ZTeam's own (its launcher reticle with a drop ladder); ZTeam's picture of a different
  weapon on that HUD left out;
- no picture of this weapon is shown (ZTeam's pack has none): its name is written in its place.

What the rifle needs of the game (materials, its melee and melee response, drop and ammunition pickup sounds, the
stock rounds panel) it names, and the map the player owns provides: the pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the
game's.

Kind: unknown. ZTeam's models are made for Custom Edition (their own nodes, a Custom Edition polygon budget) and
recreate a later game's weapon; nothing in the release says whether they and the sounds were modelled and recorded
by ZTeam or taken from that game, so this pack is not yet classed as a community model or a direct port.

Thank you, ZTeam.

AE SPIKER (weapon pack ae_spiker) - credits

The spiker's model, first-person model and animations, textures, shaders, HUD, effects and sounds, and its weapon,
spike and damage, are the work of CMT (the Custom Mapping Team), from the stock-compatible source release of The Silent
Cartographer: Evolved 1.4 (the Evolved team: Teh Lag, Ifafudafi, Siliconmaster and the contributors their credits.txt
names): CMT's multiplayer spiker, spiker_mp. They are used under the release's own terms ("Just don't take credit for
things you didn't make") and are unchanged but for what an Xbox map needs: the models converted, the multipurpose
maps' channels reordered, the high-resolution HUD made the Xbox's, a flesh impact sound's Ogg Vorbis samples encoded
again as Xbox ADPCM, and one shader's detail map, which names a campaign bitmap no multiplayer map has, left empty.

The weapon's numbers are AE's own, not CMT's: a close-to-mid automatic, 10 spikes a second and a magazine of 40 as
CMT's, 10 a spike at x0.85 on shields and x1.3 on the body (15 spikes kill from full), a faster spike (50 slowing to
30 units a second) carried 60 units with CMT's arc, a tighter first spike, aim assist at the assault rifle's reach,
and a pad pulse with each spike. The held weapon's collision and melee are the stock assault rifle's, left to the map.

The pack holds no pixel, sound or model of the game's own: what the spiker needs of the game (impact materials,
decals, particles, sounds and a reflection map) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

The pickup prompt's and kill feed's picture is CMT's own, from CMT's pickup icon sheet (hudv4.1).

Kind: community model. CMT's attribution note in the release says its models, textures and shaders are CMT's own
and that "the only non-custom assets in this release (i.e., from Halo games other than CE) are certain music and sfx
files": the spiker's models, animations and textures are CMT's. Which sounds those are the note does not say; the
spiker's firing and impact sounds may be some of them.

Thank you, CMT.

AE HJ MAGNUM (weapon pack ae_magnum_scoped) - credits

The HJ magnum's models (the black slide magnum with its rail attachments, third-person and first-person),
shaders, textures, first-person and third-person animations, collision, sounds and HUD (its ammunition meter,
scope and picture of the magnum) are ZTeam's, from the ZTeam tag
pack (released on halomaps.org). The pack names no individual authors, so none are named here. They are used as
released, but for what an Xbox map needs: the models converted to the Xbox model format, the multipurpose maps'
channels reordered.

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the weapon's numbers (magazine, rate of fire, error, zoom) and the damage of a round, set to our own values;
- the round and the firing effect are the stock pistol's with our damage and range and ZTeam's shot sound;
- ZTeam's flashlight left out; the HUD's rounds panel is the map's stock one (ZTeam's chain of panels ends in a
  full-screen visor frame);
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture is ZTeam's own picture of the magnum, resampled onto a sprite sheet (nothing
  drawn);
- the firing kick is the stock pistol's with a rumble.

The unzoomed aiming reticle is CMT's, from the pistol HUD of the stock-compatible release of The Silent Cartographer:
Evolved's source (the reticle sheet unchanged; the Xbox-map scale CMT's HUD needs applied). ZTeam's reticles are not used.
Thank you, CMT.

The pack's round and firing effect are built from the stock pistol's tags (no pixel, sound or model of the game's):
what else the magnum needs of the game (the stock rounds panel, ammunition pickup, melee, impacts, materials,
muzzle flash) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

Kind: unknown. ZTeam's models are made for Custom Edition (their own nodes, a Custom Edition polygon budget) and
recreate a later game's sidearm; nothing in the release says whether they and the sounds were modelled and
recorded by ZTeam or taken from that game, so this pack is not yet classed as a community model or a direct port.

Thank you, ZTeam.

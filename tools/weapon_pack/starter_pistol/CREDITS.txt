AE PROTOMAG (weapon pack ae_starter_pistol) - credits

The pistol's models (third-person and first-person), animations, collision, shaders, textures, sounds, firing and
reload effects, round and damage effects are the Digsite mod's 00_e3 pistol, from the official Halo CE
Mod Tools (the Halo Editing Kit's tags, digsite/weapons/pistol/00_e3 and the shared bitmaps and sounds it names).
Digsite's team is credited with thanks; the tags name no individual authors, so none are named here. They are used as
released, but for what an Xbox map needs: the models converted to the Xbox model format, the multipurpose maps'
channels reordered, the first-person animation sounds encoded again as Xbox ADPCM.

Made for Arena Evolved:
- the weapon's numbers (magazine, rate of fire, error, zoom, aim assist, ranges) and the damage of a round, set to
  our own values; the round's range shortened;
- the HUD is the map's stock rounds panel, aiming reticles and zoom mask in place of Digsite's suit interface,
  ammunition bar and ring reticle;
- the round is a real one fired from the camera (Digsite's trigger flags it client-side only and offsets it);
- the swap and pickup prompts' picture is a side view drawn from the model's geometry (nothing drawn by hand,
  nothing of the game's HUD);
- the firing kick is Digsite's with a lighter rumble.

What else the pistol needs of the game (the stock rounds panel, ammunition pickup, melee, impacts, materials, muzzle
flash, particles) it names, and the map the player owns provides.

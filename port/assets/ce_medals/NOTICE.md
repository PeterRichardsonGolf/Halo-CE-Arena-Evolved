# Campaign medal pictures

The 38 pictures here are Halo Content Evolved's campaign medal icons (Watanoge, AGPL-3.0-or-later; its credits name
halo.bungie.org/misc/halo3_hiresmedals/ for the medals), 96 by 96, 8-bit RGBA. A file's name is the medal's key
(port/linux/game/campaign_medals.h). tools/ae_ce_art.py embeds them; the end-of-mission card draws them for a player whose
MCC install has no picture of its own. Credited in port/assets/credits.txt.

# Cori (voice pack)

A voice pack for the callouts (`game.callout_voice`).

- The voice is a Qwen3-TTS 1.7B Base clone (Qwen3-TTS by the Qwen team, Apache-2.0) of Cori Samuel's public-domain
  LibriVox reading of "Living Alone" by Stella Benson.
- The clips were made for Halo CE: Arena Evolved and are released under CC0; the beeps are CC0.
- It replaces the earlier Piper-made Cori pack; that pack's builder is still in `tools/voices/` (optional).

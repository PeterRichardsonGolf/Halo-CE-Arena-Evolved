# Harper (voice pack)

A voice pack for the callouts (`game.callout_voice`): Harper, a British voice.

- Spoken by Qwen3-TTS 1.7B (Qwen team, Apache-2.0), cloned from a reference spoken by the Piper voice
  `en_GB-cori-high` (public domain, trained on public-domain LibriVox recordings).
- The clips were made for Halo CE: Arena Evolved and are released under CC0; the beeps are the Cori pack's (CC0).

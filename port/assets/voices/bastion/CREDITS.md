# Bastion (voice pack)

A voice pack for the callouts (`game.callout_voice`): an old-generation AI construct.

- The voice was designed from a text description with Qwen3-TTS 1.7B VoiceDesign and spoken with its Base model
  (Qwen3-TTS by the Qwen team, Apache-2.0). It is no real person's voice.
- The clips were made for Halo CE: Arena Evolved and are released under CC0; the beeps are the Cori pack's (CC0).

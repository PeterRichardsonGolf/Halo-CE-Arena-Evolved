# Skull pictures

The 40 pictures here are Halo Content Evolved's skull icons (Watanoge, AGPL-3.0-or-later), taken as they ship there, 96 by 96,
8-bit RGBA. A file's name is its skull's key (port/linux/include/halo_skulls.h). tools/ae_skull_art.py embeds them; the skulls
screen draws them for a player whose MCC install has no pictures of its own to read. Credited in port/assets/credits.txt.

# YOU NEED YOUR OWN HALO FILES
Arena Evolved includes none of the game's maps, sounds or art. It plays from your own copy of Halo: Combat Evolved, from a Steam install of the Master Chief Collection with its Halo CE content, or from an Xbox disc image you own.
